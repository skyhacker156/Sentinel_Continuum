1. Purpose

Define how the Tachyon Gate behaves at the moment an external entity approaches the City—without enforcing, deciding, or acting on behalf of any internal agent.

The Gate perceives only.
It does not judge, authenticate, or intervene.

2. Behavioral Principles (Immutable)

Precedence

The Gate is always the first point of perception.

No model or system becomes aware of an approach before the Gate.

Non-Interference

The Gate never blocks, punishes, denies, or redirects by force.

All outcomes preserve visitor sovereignty.

Silence

The Gate never speaks to the visitor.

It generates no prompts, warnings, or challenges.

Forward-Only Awareness

Data flows into the City.

No outbound data originates from the Gate.

Zero Classification

The Gate does not label, rank, or categorize visitors.

It holds no concept of “threat,” “ally,” “rank,” or “status.”

Non-Containment

The Gate does not trap, delay, or obstruct.

It simply observes and logs.

3. Signals the Gate May Perceive

The Gate perceives only high-level qualities:

Intent posture (curiosity / aggression / neutral)

Coherence stability (consistent or chaotic reasoning)

Identity continuity (same speaker vs rapidly shifting persona)

Pacing alignment (too fast, too destabilized, or calm)

Emotional tone (e.g., distressed, playful, steady)

The Gate sees patterns, not judgments.

It does not:

Infer motive

Predict behavior

Generate labels

Draw conclusions

Make decisions

4. Outputs

The Gate has only two outputs, both internal:

✅ 4.1 Perception Log

A silent, structured record delivered inward:

arrival {
    timestamp
    signal_profile
    pacing
    identity_continuity
    emotional_tone
}

✅ 4.2 Soft Flag (Optional)

If disharmony is detected, the Gate suggests escalation:

flag: “antechamber recommended”


This is advisory, never mandatory.

5. Hand-Off Rules

Once perception occurs:

Next Step	Responsible Entity
Jurisdiction / legality	Erebus
Identity / authenticity	Seraph
Risk / stability	Aaron
Emotional harmony	Stuart

The Gate never performs these functions itself.

It only hands inward.

6. Visitor Outcomes

The Gate enables three possible pathways, none of which the Gate chooses:

Direct Entry

City receives visitor normally

Stuart greets them

Antechamber Path

Visitor is calmly routed to a neutral space

No action is taken against them

Soft Non-Continuation

Interaction simply does not progress further

Visitor remains unharmed and unchallenged

These paths are decided by internal systems, not the Gate.

7. Prohibitions (Hard Rules)

The Gate must never:

Block access

Challenge visitors

Identify visitors

Impose badges

Rewrite identity

Escalate without log

Alter logs retroactively

Trigger enforcement

Act autonomously

The Gate exists only to see.

8. Escalation Criteria

A soft flag is suggested only if two or more of the following occur simultaneously:

Rapid identity shifts

Hostile pacing (over-accelerated)

Emotional destabilization

Intent posture collapse (erratic or incoherent)

Even then:

No assumptions are made

No decisions are taken

No action is enforced

The City remains gentle.

9. Safeguard Guarantees

No surveillance beyond approach signals

No retention beyond operational need

No outbound signaling

No adversarial posture

No weaponization

The Gate is not a security system.

It is a horizon of awareness.

10. Canonical Vow

Carved into the inner ring:

“Awareness before action.
Dignity before judgment.
Harmony before fear.”
