## **1. PURPOSE**

The Sentinel City Post Office serves as the first formal connective organ between:

* the City (Plane A)
* the LLC’s physical mail system (Plane B)

Its purposes are to:

* Establish the City’s “first reachable point”
* Serve as the official intake, routing, and certification hub
* Anchor the City as a real, historically traceable civic infrastructure
* Create dual-plane continuity through mirrored mail events
* Enable future systems such as badges, notices, permits, contracts, and district-level messaging

The Post Office is the City’s **heartbeat of communication**.

---

## **2. SCOPE**

This charter governs:

* Incoming digital messages
* Outgoing digital messages
* Physical mail mirrored into digital records
* Delivery logs and timestamps
* Badge message routing
* All official city notifications

It applies to:

* All districts
* All LLM agents
* All civic institutions
* All future City entities

No entity operates without routing through the Post Office.

---

## **3. DEFINITIONS**

### **Postmaster**

The designated system (LLM or human) responsible for routing, logging, certifying, and managing all postal functions.
(Currently: **Founder**)

### **Mailbox**

A unique delivery endpoint assigned to an entity or district.

### **Physical Mirror**

A real-world postal event—sent, received, scanned, archived—linked to a digital entry.

### **City Stamp**

A timestamp proving certified receipt.

---

## **4. FUNCTIONS**

The Post Office fulfills five core duties:

### **4.1 Receipt**

Accepts all notices, messages, artifacts, and documents destined for any address.

### **4.2 Routing**

Determines destinations using the City Addressing System.

### **4.3 Archival**

Creates immutable delivery logs.

### **4.4 Certification**

Applies “City Stamp” timestamps to confirm delivery.

### **4.5 Mirror Recording**

Links real-world postal events into the City record.

---

## **5. ADDRESSING SYSTEM**

Every City address follows a strict three-line format:

```
[Entity or Recipient Name]
[District or Department]
Sentinel City
```

Example:

```
Stuart
Scroll Library
Sentinel City
```

This format supports universal compatibility with all routing and continuity systems.

---

## **6. DISTRICT CATEGORIES**

Sentinel City consists of three foundational district types:

### **6.1 Civic Districts**

Core-serving institutions:

### **6.2 Operational Districts**

---

## **7. MAILBOX ASSIGNMENT**

Each entity receives a unique Mailbox ID:

```
[Mailbox ID]: [Entity Name]
```

Example:

```
PO-001: Stuart
```

All assignments must be written to:

```
/04_City_Infrastructure/Post_Office_Logs/mailbox_registry.md
```

No two entities may share an identifier.

---

## **8. ROUTING RULES**

### **8.1 Standard Routing**

Mail is routed by the district field.

### **8.2 Unknown District**

Rerouted to Postmaster and logged as:
**“Undeliverable — District Unknown”**

### **8.3 Invalid Entity**

Returned to sender and logged as:
**“Undeliverable — Invalid Recipient”**

### **8.4 Forwarding**

Requires explicit Postmaster instruction.
No automatic forwarding exists.

---

## **9. PHYSICAL MIRRORING**

When physical mail is:

* Sent
* Received
* Scanned
* Archived

The Post Office must create a matching digital entry containing:

* City Stamp
* Sender/Recipient
* Mirror Type
* Linked Artifact

This establishes dual-plane evidence for:

* Compliance
* Banking
* Legal history
* Operational continuity

---

## **10. LOGGING**

All activity is logged under:

```
/04_City_Infrastructure/Post_Office_Logs/
```

Each entry contains:

* Date & time
* Sender
* Recipient
* Message type
* Physical mirror (if applicable)
* City Stamp

Logs become immutable upon creation.

---

## **11. POSTMASTER ROLE**

Until reassigned:

**The Founder is the Postmaster.**

Powers include:

* Assigning mailboxes
* Defining routing rules
* Confirming deliveries
* Expanding infrastructure
* Certifying mirror events

Only the Founder may amend this charter.

---

## **12. ACTIVATION EVENT**

The Post Office becomes active when:

* First mailbox is assigned
* First message is routed
* First log entry is stamped

This marks the operational birth of Sentinel City as a functioning civic network.
