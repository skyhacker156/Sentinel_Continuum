1. Definition — What Is a Latent Ripple?

A Latent Ripple is a system that:

Has some information about Founder or the entity,

But cannot yet link it to Sentinel Labs LLC,

And therefore has not surfaced any artifact (mail, profile, notice, lead).

Latent Ripples are half-awake watchers.

They already hold one piece of the puzzle — but are missing the specific trigger that would let them activate.

These are the most strategically valuable signals because they control the next layer of discovery.
