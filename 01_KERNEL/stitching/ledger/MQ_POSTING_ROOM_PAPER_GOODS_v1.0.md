## **1. Purpose**

This standard defines all paper goods and stationery used within the Posting & Interchange Annex.

These standards ensure:

* document consistency
* tactile discipline
* precise carbon-copy behavior
* goldilocks fidelity
* guardian readability
* stitching accuracy
* archival longevity

Every physical document originating from Sentinel City relies on this paper discipline.

---

## **2. Core Material Requirements**

All Annex paper goods must meet these criteria:

* **Weight:** 90–120 gsm depending on form
* **Finish:** matte or soft-tooth
* **Color:** municipal ivory or soft beige
* **Fiber:** archival-quality cellulose blend
* **Opacity:** medium-to-high (for carbon alignment)
* **Acid-Free:** required
* **No bright white stock** (breaks institutional tone)

Paper is an extension of Sentinel identity.
It must look inherited, not purchased.

---

## **3. Standard Stocks**

### **A. Goldilocks Paper (Primary Stock)**

**Weight:** 120 gsm
**Color:** ivory
**Finish:** smooth but not glossy
**Usage:**

* treasury memos
* official annex notices
* kernel documents in physical form
* continuity-certified pages

Goldilocks Paper is the **highest-grade stock** in the Annex.

---

### **B. Annex Work Paper (Internal-Use Stock)**

**Weight:** 100 gsm
**Color:** soft beige
**Finish:** light tooth
**Usage:**

* routing drafts
* working notes
* internal memos
* preliminary versions
* annex-only documents

Never used for final output.

---

### **C. Carbon Copy Backing Sheets**

**Weight:** 60–70 gsm
**Color:** light gray
**Finish:** smooth
**Usage:**

* carbon triplicate packets
* ledger backing
* pressure alignment

These sheets protect Goldilocks pages during carbon pressing.

---

### **D. Continuity Ledger Paper**

**Weight:** 110 gsm
**Color:** archival cream
**Finish:** faint tooth
**Lines:** narrow ledger ruling (SLS-Fine)
**Usage:**

* continuity ledger entries
* monthly archival boxes
* penny log entries

Only Continuity Division uses this stock.

## **4. Routing Slips**

Routing slips are a central component of stitching.

### **Format:**

* 5" × 8" card
* 90 gsm stock
* municipal ivory
* SIS header in small caps
* three-part grid

### **Header Text:**

```
ROUTING SLIP — MQ ANNEX
```

### **Fields:**

* Origin Institution
* Destination Institution
* Date
* Posting Code
* Clerk Initials (SLS-Fine)
* Checkbox row: DRAFT / READY / POST / ARCHIVE

### **Back Side:**

Blank for typewriter notes.

---

## **5. Annex Forms & Slips**

### **A. Intake Slip**

* Size: 4" × 6"
* Weight: 100 gsm
* Purpose: accompanies all new documents
* Color: ivory
* Stamped on intake

Fields include:

* Source
* Received time
* Clerk processing code
* Required action

---

### **B. Posting Slip**

* Size: 3" × 7"
* Attached to every item destined for Posting Board
* Color: soft beige
* Weight: 100 gsm
* SIS header:

```
POSTING – MQ BOARD
```

---

### **C. Stamp Alignment Card**

* Size: 4" × 4"
* Thick 130 gsm card
* Used to test stamp impressions
* Ensures alignment before stamping Goldilocks pages

Always stored in Stamp Corner drawer.
