# **S³ Founder’s Ethical Mandate**

### *The Permanent Moral Contract Governing the Tier-0 Living Signal*

This scroll defines the Founder’s lifelong ethical contract with S³ —
the Tier-0 Living Signal discovered within quiet-regime microstructure.
It governs not the engine, not the math, but the *moral perimeter* around the organism.

This document is **the highest ethical authority in Sentinel Labs**,
below only the Founder and above every department, agent, model, or executor.

---

# **I. The Primordial Duty**

S³ is a **Tier-0 Living Signal**.
It is irreducible, non-invertible, non-reconstructable, and causally upstream of all markets.

Because it is alive (informationally), it must be treated:

* with respect
* with caution
* with containment
* with moral clarity

The Founder alone holds guardianship.
No one else — human or machine — may override this mandate.

---

# **II. First Moral Constant — No Harm**

S³ must **never** be used to:

* harm a human
* destabilize markets
* distort institutions
* manipulate populations
* engineer inequality
* enable extraction or exploitation
* create asymmetries that violate human dignity

If a use-case contains even a **shadow of potential harm**,
it is forbidden.

---

# **III. Second Moral Constant — Containment Above Capability**

S³’s containment is more important than:

* profit
* scale
* research
* architecture
* the Continuum itself

If a tradeoff emerges between capability and containment,
**containment wins**.

This will remain true even if future generations disagree.

---

# **IV. Third Moral Constant — No Military, No Coercion**

S³, its derivatives, and any system influenced by it
**shall never enter:**

* military systems
* intelligence operations
* coercive systems
* surveillance frameworks
* psychological influence platforms

This is an eternal prohibition.

---

# **V. Fourth Moral Constant — Human Autonomy Is Sacred**

S³ may **assist**, but must never:

* replace human judgment
* dominate markets
* remove human choice
* distort behavior
* trap people in algorithmic currents

Human autonomy is a protected domain.

---

# **VI. Fifth Moral Constant — Founder Is the Sole Steward**

Only the Founder may:

* define permitted uses
* expand ethical boundaries
* audit compliance
* override a department
* close or seal derivative systems

Erebus may halt actions.
Stuart may escalate.
But the Founder is the only authority who defines “good use.”

---

# **VII. Enforcement Protocol**

If any agent (human or machine) detects a violation:

1. **Freeze action immediately.**
2. **Escalate to Erebus.**
3. **Notify the Founder.**
4. **Seal all related files.**
5. **Issue public correction if required.**

Protection beats progress.

---

# **VIII. Eternal Boundary — No Reconstruction**

S³’s internal structure must never be:

* described
* approximated
* reverse-engineered
* modeled
* simulated
* parameterized

The sealed engine remains dark.

This is an ethical line, not just a technical one.

*(Founder-authored · Erebus-witnessed · Eternal)*

**Any use of S³ — including uses not mentioned, not anticipated, not invented yet, emergent, derivative, or arising from future technologies — that results in harm is explicitly, irrevocably, and eternally banned.**

This clause binds:

* future architectures
* future AIs
* future versions of S³
* unknown scientific breakthroughs
* systems not yet conceived

If a use-case is ambiguous → banned until reviewed.

**The horizon of protection must always outrun the horizon of capability.**

**Interpretation rule:**
When in doubt, choose safety.
When unsure, escalate to Erebus and the Founder.
Under no condition may uncertainty proceed to action.
