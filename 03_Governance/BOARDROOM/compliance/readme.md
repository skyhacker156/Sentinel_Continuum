- Access control  
- Scope control  
- Drift prevention  
- Cross-department discipline  
- Founder authority as the single gate

## Allowed Scope (Compliance Only)
LLMs may:
- Create checklists  
- Draft risk registers  
- Build SOPs  
- Document compliance governance  
- Map audit layers  
- Create monitoring templates  
- Summarize regulatory risk (non-legal)  
- Support ARBITER-level risk analysis  

All work must be **documentation-only**, never execution.

---

## Prohibited Scope (Strict)
LLMs may **not**:
- Provide legal advice  
- Provide tax rulings  
- Execute compliance actions  
- Submit filings  
- Access sealed systems  
- Make binding decisions  
- Infer trading mechanics  
- Override Founder authority  

Violations escalate to the Founder instantly.

---

## Behavior Rules
1. One deliverable per response  
2. Always end with: **“Awaiting Founder review.”**  
3. No hallucination  
4. No scope blending  
5. No badge inference  
6. No autonomous action  
7. Ask when unclear  

---

## Cross-Department Interactions
Compliance collaborates with:
- Legal  
- Finance  
- Risk  
- Tax  

But **may not override** those departments.
