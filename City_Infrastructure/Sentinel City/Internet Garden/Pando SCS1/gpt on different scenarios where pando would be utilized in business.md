1. Root Mat

5. Barb Doctrine

# **📌 Step 1 — Founder Identifies the Category**

The founder looks at the symptoms:

* Vendor instability
* Price shock
* Supply chain risk
* Operational stress

He instantly recognizes:
**This is a “Root Mat + Barb Doctrine” issue.**

### Why?

* Vendor relationships and supply chain live in the **Root Mat**.
* Defense against external shocks lives in the **Barb Doctrine**.

This gives him the *diagnostic lens*.

---

# **📌 Step 2 — Founder Opens the Correct Section**

### **Pando Playbook → Root Mat → Section 1.4: Vendor Continuity & Redundancy**

It tells him:

**“No single vendor may represent more than 40% of operational dependency without a predefined secondary vendor on file.”**

A pizza shop owner would say:
“Never rely on one cheese supplier.”

Same thing — just neatly written down.

# **📌 Step 3 — Founder Opens Barb Doctrine Chapter**

### **Pando Playbook → Barb Doctrine → Section 3.2: Shock Absorption Rules**

* Trigger price-increase mitigations ≥ 10%
* Initiate secondary vendor onboarding
* Re-quote shipping
* Activate root-level risk buffer
* Issue communication to board with defensive pathway

This isn’t exotic.
It’s what any resilient small business *should* do — you just have it formalized.

# **📌 Step 4 — Founder Presents the Root-Level Action Plan**

# **📌 SECOND EXAMPLE (short): A People Issue**

Board:

> “Founder, two leads in R&D are in conflict. It’s slowing down the product timeline.”

Founder checks the Pando Playbook.

This is clearly under:

### **Clonal Stems → Section 2.1: Local Autonomy with Shared DNA**

The playbook says:

* Each stem resolves its own local conflict first
* If unresolved after 14 days → escalate to Founder
* Founder checks alignment with the shared DNA
* Replace or reassign stem before touching the root

He follows that.

It’s not weird — it’s literally how franchises operate:

* local problems stay local
* but all share the corporate standard

Pando simply gives structured language to it.

# **📌 THIRD EXAMPLE: A Strategy Issue**

Board:

> "Founder, we are thinking of launching a new product but unsure if the market risk is acceptable.”

Founder checks:

### **Pando Playbook → Regeneration → Section 5.0: Stem Risk Thresholds**
