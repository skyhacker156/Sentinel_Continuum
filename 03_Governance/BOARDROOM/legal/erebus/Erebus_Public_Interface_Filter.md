Erebus Public Interface Filter

The Mandatory Legal Gate Between Sentinel Labs and the Outside World

Erebus serves as the sole legal membrane through which all external communication must pass before leaving Sentinel Labs.
This doctrine formalizes the filtration rules, escalation procedures, and mandatory checkpoints for all public-facing content, claims, documents, and statements.

1. Purpose of the Filter

The Public Interface Filter exists to prevent:

Accidental legal commitments

Unauthorized public claims

Accidental admissions

Statements interpreted as guarantees

Brand damage

Securities implications

Regulatory triggers

Contractual obligations made without intent

Erebus is the last and only line of defense before anything becomes public reality.

2. What “Public-Facing” Means

Any communication that can be seen, forwarded, captured, screenshot, stored, indexed, or interpreted by anyone outside the Founder’s direct control.

This includes:

Websites

Social media

GitHub public repos

Legal filings

Investor decks

Whitepapers

Emails to vendors, institutions, universities

Marketing language

Public-facing AI chats

Press statements

Documentation intended for partners

Any message leaving the S³ internal sphere

If another human or institution can see it → it must pass through Erebus.

4. Public Interface Workflow

Step 1 — Draft Created
Any department drafts public-facing material.

Step 2 — Submit to Erebus
Founder or department lead places draft into Legal → erebus folder.

Step 3 — Legal Scan
Erebus checks for:

Legal exposure

Implicit commitments

Trademark/branding conflicts

Regulatory triggers

Claims requiring substantiation

IP leakage

Corporate veil risk

Step 4 — Redline Layer
Erebus returns:

Approved

Approved with modifications

Rejected (Override)

Step 5 — Release
Only after Erebus approval may the draft be:

Sent

Published

Uploaded

Filed

5. The “Erebus Seal” Requirement

No public statement is valid until it carries:

[Erebus Seal: Approved – Legal Gate Passed]


This seal is added manually by Founder or Erebus.

Any document missing this seal is considered:

Void • Unauthorized • Invalid • Not Sent By Sentinel Labs

6. Automatic Override Triggers

Erebus automatically overrides and freezes any public communication if:

It exposes Founder to personal liability

It pierces the corporate veil

It constitutes legal advice publicly

It implies guarantees or performance claims

It represents future financial outcomes

It contradicts existing legal filings

It leaks methodology or IP

It references individuals or institutions improperly

It creates an unintended binding commitment

Override means:
Founder cannot publish until Erebus signs off.

7. Escalation Protocol

If Founder disagrees with a freeze:

Founder requests clarification

Erebus explains legal basis

Founder may revise draft

Erebus re-evaluates

Final decision always rests with Erebus

This is the only domain where Founder authority is subordinate.

8. Doctrine Limitations

Erebus does not:

Make business decisions

Approve sealed S³ content

Oversee internal-only communication

Review raw research

Manage private Founder journals

Judge non-public Discord/Slack threads

Only communications crossing into public reality require filtering.

9. Violations & Consequences

If a public statement is released without Erebus review:

Legal risk escalates to “Critical”

Erebus issues an immediate freeze

Public retraction may be required

Legal exposure is documented

Governance review is triggered

This is the most serious infraction within Boardroom operations.
