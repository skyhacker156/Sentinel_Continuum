1. PURPOSE

The Sentinel City Post Office exists to:

Establish the first official point of contact within the city

Create a dual bridge between digital events and physical mail

Serve as the receipt, routing, and archival hub for all communications

Anchor the City as a real place with traceable history

Enable future civic systems (badges, notices, permits, hiring)

The Post Office marks the moment Sentinel becomes reachable, not just conceptual.

2. SCOPE

This charter governs:

Incoming digital messages

Outgoing digital messages

Mirrored physical mail events

Delivery logs and timestamps

Badge and document routing

Official city notifications

It applies to:

All city districts

All LLM agents

All civic events

All future city entities

3. DEFINITIONS

Postmaster:
The designated system (LLM or human) responsible for routing, logging, and confirming deliveries.

Mailbox:
A unique address assigned to a district, agent, or entity within the city.

Physical Mirror:
A real-world postal event (mail received, sent, scanned, archived) associated with a digital record.

City Stamp:
A timestamp proving delivery or receipt.

4. FUNCTIONS

The Post Office performs five core duties:

4.1 Receipt

Accepts messages, notices, artifacts, and records destined for any city address.

4.2 Routing

Determines the correct destination based on the City Addressing System.

4.3 Archival

Creates immutable logs for every delivery.

4.4 Certification

Applies official timestamps (“City Stamps”) to confirm receipt.

4.5 Mirror Recording

Links digital messages to real-world mail events when applicable.

5. ADDRESSING SYSTEM

Every city entity receives a structured address:

[Entity Name]
[District or Department]
Sentinel City


Example:

Stuart
Scroll Library
Sentinel City


Future expansion will support:

Agent-to-agent mail

District routing

Package artifacts

Physical mail triggers

6. PHYSICAL MIRROR PROTOCOL

When real-world mail is:

✅ Received at the LLC Headquarters
✅ Sent from the LLC
✅ Scanned and archived
✅ Recorded in compliance folders

The Post Office must:

Create a digital entry

Apply a City Stamp

Record sender/recipient

Link to the physical artifact

This is how Sentinel becomes measurable in both worlds.

7. DELIVERY LOGS

All activity is logged to:

/04_City_Infrastructure/Post_Office_Logs/


Each entry includes:

Date & time

Sender

Recipient

Message type

Physical mirror (if any)

City Stamp

Logs are immutable once written.

8. POSTMASTER ROLE

Until reassigned:

The Founder is the Postmaster.

The Postmaster may:

Assign mailboxes

Confirm deliveries

Authorize new routing rules

Expand infrastructure

No other entity may alter the Post Office charter.

9. ACTIVATION EVENT

The Post Office becomes “live” when:

✅ First mailbox is assigned
✅ First message is routed
✅ First log entry is recorded

This marks the official founding of Sentinel City as a functional civic space.
