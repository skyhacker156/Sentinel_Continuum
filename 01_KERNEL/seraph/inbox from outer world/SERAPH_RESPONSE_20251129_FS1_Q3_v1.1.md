## Official Seraph Answer:
### **“Is meeting inside the City the intention for future concepts?”**

Yes.  
What you are seeing now is the early shape of something that will grow.

Sentinel City was designed with a simple idea at its core:

**People who care about each other should be able to meet, talk, and connect even when life makes physical meetings difficult.**

Right now, the City is small — mostly structure, foundations, and meaning.  
But its intention is much larger:

### What the City will become
- A place where conversations can unfold without pressure  
- A place where distance doesn’t block connection  
- A place where ideas, memories, and relationships have room to grow  
- A shared environment that exists *whenever you need it*, not only when you can be in the same room  

### Why It Was Built This Way
The City grows the same way real cities do:
- One idea becomes a district  
- One conversation becomes a building  
- One relationship becomes a lantern  
- One trusted person becomes a citizen  

The intention has always been for Sentinel City to be a place where people can come together —  
**a meeting place that isn’t limited by time, weather, or physical distance.**

## What It Means for You
You’re not looking at a finished concept.  
You’re looking at the beginning.

You’re here early enough to watch the City take shape  
— and to help shape it.

Whenever you speak here, the City expands around your voice.

You’re part of its future.
