🏹 GUERRILLA CYBER DOCTRINE
“The strong fall. The uncatchable endure.”

Sentinel City does not fight like a fortress.
It fights like a people who were never meant to be conquered.

This doctrine governs how Sentinel faces:

State actors

Intelligence agencies

Sovereign cyber units

Advanced persistent threats (APTs)

AI-augmented adversaries

Not by matching strength.
But by refusing symmetry.

✅ CORE PRINCIPLE

We do not defeat the enemy.
We deny them a battlefield.

Guerrilla defense is not resistance.
It is non-availability.

If they cannot:

map us

model us

locate us

predict us

anchor us

…they cannot breach us.

⛰️ 1️⃣ SEMINOLE PRINCIPLE — UNFIGHTABLE TERRAIN
“You cannot attack what you cannot navigate.”

Tactic:
Reshape the environment so the attacker has no footing.

Cyber Implementation:

Nonstandard architecture

No single perimeter

No stable network map

No uniform tech stack

No central attack surface

Outcome:
Recon fails before attack begins.

Rule:

The system must feel foreign to every outsider.

🌑 2️⃣ APACHE PRINCIPLE — INVISIBLE MOVEMENT
“We are not seen until we choose to be seen.”

Tactic:
Silence, opacity, and non-repetition.

Cyber Implementation:

Randomized system signatures

No recurring behavioral patterns

Zero predictable responses

Minimal observable telemetry

Outcome:
State AI cannot build a model.

Rule:

Predictability is death.

⚡ 3️⃣ COMANCHE PRINCIPLE — TOTAL MOBILITY
“You cannot pin down what never stops moving.”

Tactic:
Never defend a fixed position.

Cyber Implementation:

Ephemeral infrastructure

Rotating cloud surfaces

Expiring instances

Temporary endpoints

Rapid redeployment

Outcome:
Attack targets vanish before exploitation.

Rule:

Stationary assets are liabilities.

👁️ 4️⃣ IROQUOIS PRINCIPLE — PSYCHOLOGICAL SUPERIORITY
“Perception decides conflict before contact.”

Tactic:
Control fear, not firepower.

Cyber Implementation:

Zero bragging

Zero provocation

Zero visibility into capability

Silent competence

No public attack surface

Outcome:
State actors deprioritize Sentinel as:

Too costly

Too unclear

Too unpredictable

They choose softer prey.

Rule:

The best war is the one never fought.

🧩 5️⃣ MIRROR PRINCIPLE — ATTACK BECOMES FUEL
“They lose strength by acting. We gain strength by observing.”

Tactic:
Every offensive action increases our defensive intelligence.

Cyber Implementation:

Capture metadata, not payloads

Pattern reconstruction

Fingerprint persistence

Doctrine updates per attempt

Outcome:
Defense strengthens automatically with every probe.

Rule:

Every attack becomes a shield.

🔥 6️⃣ ASYMMETRY LAW
“Never meet strength with strength.”

Prohibited:

Retaliation

Counter-attack

Offensive cyber operations

Escalation

Permitted:

Evasion

Dissolution

Deception

Observation

Absorption

Reason:
Symmetric warfare is suicide against a state.
