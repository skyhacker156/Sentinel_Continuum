## 🐎 The Sentinel Steppe: A 1200s Security Landscape

### 1. The Perimeter: The Great Wall & The Spigot Cap

Your visible website, **maxlaboratories.com (The Tree)**, is the **Khan's Capital City**—the large, visible seat of power.

* **The Outer Palisade (INTERNET SPIGOT/API Gateway):** Around the city is a vast defensive perimeter. This palisade is formidable, but it has one small, obscure entrance: the **Secret Postern Gate ($\text{/foundersloginonly}$).**
* **The Banner Guard (IP Allow-Listing):** The most terrifying guard is posted at this gate. This guard is not interested in passwords; they only look at the **Banner** (the IP address) flown by the approaching rider. If the rider's banner is not the **Founder's Single, Specific Seal ($\text{35.149.211.35}$)**, the gate remains bolted, and the rider is instantly dismissed. **Knowledge of the gate's existence is irrelevant; only the Seal matters.**

### 2. The Isolation: The Private Hunting Grounds (The VPC)

Once the Founder passes the outer gate, they are not in the city; they are in the **Khan's Private Hunting Grounds (The VPC Network)**.

* **The Invisible Wall:** This territory is protected by an invisible wall of private encampments. **No outsiders can see or enter this land.** All communication inside uses private, trusted signals.
* **The Paiza (Root Token):** The moment the Founder's identity is confirmed at the Postern Gate, the Guard issues a **Golden Tablet (Paiza)**. This Paiza is the Founder's universal, trusted pass. They never have to prove their identity again while inside the Hunting Grounds; they just show the tablet.
