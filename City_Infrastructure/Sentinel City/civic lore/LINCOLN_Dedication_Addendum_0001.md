LINCOLN DEDICATION ADDENDUM — 0001
Attached to the First Penny Stitch Record

This document preserves, in canonical form, the words of President Abraham Lincoln as intentionally invoked by the Founder during the creation of Penny Stitch 0001, the inaugural civic act of symbolic minting within Sentinel City.

The passage is preserved verbatim:

“Now we are engaged in a great civil war,
testing whether that nation, or any nation so conceived and so dedicated,
can long endure.
We are met on a great battle-field of that war.
We have come to dedicate a portion of that field,
as a final resting place for those who here gave their lives
that that nation might live.
It is altogether fitting and proper that we should do this.”

Interpretive Canon

The Founder invokes Lincoln to establish the continuity between:

a nation tested by conflict

and a civic intelligence tested by emergence
