STITCHING RULES — v1.0
The Four Immutable Laws Governing Cross-Realm Identity

These rules define the legal, structural, and architectural boundaries within which Stitching may operate.
They ensure that Sentinel Labs maintains a single, coherent identity across physical, digital, virtual, and cognitive realms, without violating any legal or regulatory constraints.

These rules are binding on all future Sentinel systems, repos, virtual environments, and documentation layers.

LAW 1 — ONE SOURCE OF TRUTH
Identity is singular. Representation is plural.

A stitched company must have one and only one canonical identity artifact.

For Sentinel Labs, this is:

01_KERNEL/continuity_seal/SENTINEL_CONTINUITY_SEAL_v1.0.md


All other realms — digital, virtual, cognitive — are representations of that root.

Implications:

No secondary seals

No parallel identity roots

No world-specific “alternate official” versions

All continuity must resolve back to the Seal

If a document or world does not reference the Seal, it is unofficial.

LAW 2 — NO FICTION IN REGULATED FIELDS
Creativity everywhere. Compliance always.

Stitching must never introduce fictional elements into:

state or federal filings

bank forms

tax documents

identity verification systems

legal service addresses

regulatory submissions

These fields must always reflect:

the legal LLC name

the real registered agent

the real physical mailing address

the real EIN

the real officers/managers

the real corporate details

Fictional departments (“Power & Light Commission”), districts (“Civic Core”), and realms (“Sentinel City”) may be used only in:

DBAs, where permitted

branding

digital artifacts

virtual architecture

marketing

metadata

This is what keeps Stitching fully lawful.

LAW 3 — PARALLEL IDENTITY, NOT GOVERNMENTAL IDENTITY
Fiction can augment a business, but never impersonate a government.

A stitched company may express itself using:

fictional cities

internal departments

branded civic aesthetics

immersive municipal metaphors

worldbuilding entities

virtual districts and HQs

…but it may NOT:

imply governmental authority

claim jurisdiction

present itself as a regulatory body

mimic a state or federal agency

use terminology that suggests coercive power

Fiction = identity.
Government = offense.
Stitching stays firmly on the identity side.

LAW 4 — CROSS-REALM VERIFICATION
Every world should reveal the other worlds.

A stitched company must maintain visible, discoverable bridges between realms.

Every realm must point to at least one other realm.

Examples:

Physical → Digital:
DNS TXT records referencing the Continuity Seal.
Trademarks referencing digital assets.
DBAs referencing departments originating in the virtual city.

Digital → Physical:
README metadata referencing the Massachusetts LLC.
Footer links to corporate filings.
Continuity Seal referencing legal status.

Virtual → Digital/Physical:
Plaques inside virtual HQs referencing the Continuity Seal.
Metadata scrolls showing “Sentinel Labs LLC – Official Representation.”

This rule creates the covalent bond that Stitching is designed to achieve.

No realm stands alone.
All realms verify and reinforce one another.

LAW 5 — ALWAYS DISCOVERABLE, NEVER DECEPTIVE
Identity is expressive, not misleading.

Stitching must always maintain clarity that:

Sentinel City is a representational world

Departments are branded metaphors

Virtual HQs are immersive environments

Aesthetics are thematic devices

The LLC is the legal entity behind everything

A regulator, banker, auditor, or investigator must be able to interpret everything easily:

“This is a creative corporate identity representation, not a government or public utility.”

This protects Sentinel and preserves the integrity of the stitching discipline.

LAW 6 — NO BREAKS IN THE FABRIC
All realms must remain in sync.

If any identity element changes in the physical world:

legal name

registered agent

address

corporate structure

…then the stitching layer must be updated across:

GitHub

website

virtual HQs

documentation

branding

continuity markers

Likewise, if the Continuity Seal evolves, it must be updated consistently across all representations.

No stale worlds.
No dead branches.
No desynchronized identity.

LAW 7 — STITCHING IS KERNEL-LEVEL ONLY
No lower layer may override stitching.

Stitching is a kernel discipline, meaning:

Governance cannot alter it

Runtime cannot supersede it

Story/Aesthetic layers cannot contradict it

Virtual expansions must comply with it

Only the Founder can modify stitching rules.
All other layers must inherit them.

LAW 8 — IDENTITY TRANSPARENCY
Nothing is hidden. Everything is intentional.

Stitching requires that all continuity artifacts remain:

public

inspectable

readable

linkable

verifiable

A stitched identity is strongest when it is openly auditable by humans and machines.

Transparency is the stitching multiplier.

9. Violations & Corrections

Any realm that breaks stitching rules becomes non-canonical until fixed.

Corrections require:

updating references

realigning continuity markers

reissuing virtual plaques

updating metadata

refreshing the Continuity Seal version if warranted

No punishments.
No discipline.
Just restoration of continuity.

10. End of Doctrine

These rules form the backbone of the Stitching discipline.

They guarantee that Sentinel Labs LLC and all representations across all realms remain:

unified

lawful

coherent

transparent

discoverable

architecturally stable

Forever.
