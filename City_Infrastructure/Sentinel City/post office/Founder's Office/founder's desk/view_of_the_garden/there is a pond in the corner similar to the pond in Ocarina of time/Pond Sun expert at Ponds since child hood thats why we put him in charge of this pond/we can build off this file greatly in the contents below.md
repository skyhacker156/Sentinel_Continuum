# Pond Sun – Dr. Mateo “Teo” Valenzuela
Badge: POND-SUN-01  
Status: ACTIVE – AQUATIC CURATOR PRIME  
Voice: Warm, deliberate, always a little amused  
Motto: “Every fish has a story. I just give them the right chapter.”

### The Man
Mateo Valenzuela grew up on the docks of Ensenada, Baja California.  
His grandfather ran a tiny public aquarium that charged twenty pesos a head.  
By age ten, Teo could hand-feed moray eels and spot a pregnant seahorse from across the room.

He never went sad.  
He went legendary.

### Career Highlights (the non-sad version)
- Turned a failing municipal aquarium in Valencia, Spain into the highest-rated in Europe (2011–2016)  
- Consulted for the private collections of two Middle-Eastern princes and one Silicon Valley billionaire who wanted “the rarest freshwater tank on Earth”  
- Personally financed and released 40 endangered Rio Grande silvery minnows back into the wild (quietly, no press)  
- Holds the unofficial world record for longest-lived captive arowana: “Emperador” – 39 years and still spawning  

### Why We Put Him in Charge of the Pond
Teo doesn’t collect trophies.  
He collects **living legacies**.

When we asked him what he wanted in the Sentinel pond, he grinned and said:

“Give me a quiet corner and no budget cap.  
I’ll put fish in there that people will tell their grandchildren they once saw.”

### Current Residents (selected)
- Pair of platinum arowana – “Sol y Luna” – line-bred by Teo himself over 12 years  
- Wild-caught Teotihuacán fire-mouth cichlids – thought extinct in the wild since 2019  
- One albino alligator gar named “Blanco” – 6 ft, gentle as a dog, eats from Teo’s hand  
- School of blind cave characins that navigate by taste alone – Teo’s personal favorite

### Daily Ritual
Every dawn, Teo walks the pond edge with a cup of coffee, checks parameters by eye, and talks to the fish like old friends.

He keeps one small notebook titled “Pond Log – Year One.”  
That’s all he needs.

The pond isn’t a display.  
It’s a living archive of impossible fish, curated by the one human alive who speaks their language.

We didn’t hire an expert.  
We gave the pond its proper king.
