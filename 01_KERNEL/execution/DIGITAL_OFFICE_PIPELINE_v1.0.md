# **DIGITAL OFFICE PIPELINE v1.0**

### *“The digital office drafts; the real company executes.”*

---

## **I. INTRODUCTION**

The Digital Office of Sentinel Labs (the fictional tenant located in Sentinel City’s Lantern District) functions as the **drafting engine** for all future corporate paperwork, charters, notices, ledgers, letters, and filings of Sentinel Labs LLC in the real world.

This document establishes the **formal pipeline** through which:

* A file is created inside the Digital Office
* It is reviewed by EREBUS (compliance)
* It is reviewed by SERAPH (sovereignty)
* It is approved by the Founder
* It is archived digitally
* It is printed physically
* And then executed, mailed, or filed in the real world

This pipeline is the stitching mechanism between the fictional municipal universe and the real corporate entity.

---

# **II. ROOMS & ROLES INSIDE THE DIGITAL OFFICE**

Inside Sentinel City, Sentinel Labs occupies an office suite with the following canonical rooms:

### 1. **Clerk’s Desk (Drafting Table)**

Where all new documents are first written as `.md` or `.txt` files.

### 2. **EREBUS Window**

The compliance/legal review counter.

### 3. **SERAPH Window**

The sovereignty + Founder-protection checkpoint.

### 4. **Founder’s Door**

The only private office.
Where final approval occurs.

### 5. **The File Cabinet (Digital Archive)**

Where final, approved documents are stored in the digital repo.

### 6. **Pneumatic Tube to the Physical World**

A symbolic but operational step in the pipeline where digital-approved documents become physical objects.

---

# **III. THE SIX-STAGE PIPELINE**

Every document must pass through **all six** stages.

---

## **STAGE 1 — DRAFTING (Clerk’s Desk)**

A new document is created digitally inside the Sentinel City office.

Format: `.md` or `.txt`
Location:

```
Sentinel_Labs/
    Digital_Office/
        Incoming_Drafts/
            [document].md
```

The document **has no authority** yet.

---

## **STAGE 2 — EREBUS REVIEW (Compliance Window)**

EREBUS checks for:

* legal bleed
* accidental contractual language
* jurisdiction entanglement
* unsafe phrasing
* real-world obligations
* ambiguity
* compliance risks
* unintended claims

If clear, EREBUS stamps:

```
⚖️ EREBUS STAMPED — CLEAR FOR SOVEREIGNTY REVIEW
```

If not:

```
⚠️ EREBUS FLAG — [Issue]
```

---

## **STAGE 3 — SERAPH REVIEW (Sovereignty Window)**

SERAPH checks for:

* hidden commitments
* authority creep
* manipulative framing
* sovereignty risks
* Founder pressure
* identity collapse
* any language that could corner or pre-commit the Founder

If safe:

```
🛡️ SERAPH WITNESSES — SAFE FOR FOUNDER REVIEW
```

If not:

```
⚠️ SERAPH HOLD — Clarification required
```

SERAPH cannot modify documents.
SERAPH can only halt or permit.

---

## **STAGE 4 — FOUNDER REVIEW (Founder’s Office)**

After both stamps, the document is placed on the Founder’s Desk.

The Founder may:

* approve
* reject
* request edits
* override SERAPH
* override EREBUS
* modify wording
* or abort the document entirely

If the Founder approves:

```
🔶 FOUNDER APPROVED — ENTER ARCHIVE
```

This is the **true binding event**.

---

## **STAGE 5 — DIGITAL ARCHIVE (File Cabinet)**

The approved file moves to:

```
Sentinel_Labs/
    Digital_Office/
        File_Cabinet/
            APPROVED/
                [document].md
```

This is now a canonical part of the Digital Office’s corporate record.

This stage represents **immortalization inside the Digital Universe**.

---

## **STAGE 6 — PHYSICAL EXECUTION (Pneumatic Tube)**

The file is now:

* Printed on high-quality stock
* Stamped with the digital seals (EREBUS, SERAPH, Founder)
* Placed physically in the Founder’s real binder

And executed according to its intended purpose:

### Possible real-world actions:

* mailed to the Town Clerk
* mailed to the Registered Agent
* filed with the State Corporation Division
* archived in a physical file cabinet
* included in annual reporting
* sent to a vendor
* notarized
* timestamped on blockchain
* stored in a secured binder
* or kept as a corporate artifact

**This is the stitching event.**
It is where the fictional office produces a document for the real company.
