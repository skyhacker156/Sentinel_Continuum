## LOG FIELD DEFINITIONS

**DECISION_ID**: Unique identifier assigned by Founder (e.g., D-0001, FD-2025-001, or format Founder specifies)  
- Once assigned, never changes  
- Permanent reference for audit and legal purposes

**ISSUE_DATE**: Date Founder formalized the decision (YYYY-MM-DD format)  
- This is when the decision became binding

**SOURCE_PACKET**: Reference to where decision came from  
- FINAL_INTEGRATION_SUMMARY (from Boardroom)  
- BOARDROOM_RECORD (from deliberation)  
- FOUNDER_DIRECTIVE (direct Founder decision)  
- Other reference format Founder specifies

**DECISION_TYPE**: Category of decision (Founder defines categories)  
- Suggested: Constitutional / Operational / Safety / Structural / Resource / Other  
- Used for routing and escalation

**DECISION_SUMMARY**: Concise plain-language description of what was decided  
- Not the full text, just enough to identify it  
- Public-safe language only (no Tier-0 exposure)

**EFFECTIVE_DATE**: When the decision takes effect (YYYY-MM-DD format)  
- May differ from ISSUE_DATE if deferred implementation is needed

**OWNER_ROLE**: Who is accountable for this decision's execution  
- Typically a domain lead (Finance, Operations, Security, etc.)  
- Can be Founder if not delegated

**STATUS**: Current state of the decision  
- Pending: Registered, awaiting routing  
- Notified: Sent to operational domains  
- In-Progress: Execution underway  
- Blocked: Escalation required  
- Completed: Executed successfully  
- Deferred: Placed on hold  
- Revised: Replaced by new decision  
- Closed: Archived after completion

**DEADLINE**: By when must execution be complete (YYYY-MM-DD format or "ongoing")  
- This is a hard commitment  
- If missed, triggers escalation

**NOTES**: Any clarifications, dependencies, or special instructions  
- Links to related decisions  
- Escalation events  
- Status updates  
- Changes to original decision

## HOW THE LOG WORKS

### 1. Decision Entry (Founder)

Founder makes a decision → Founder assigns DECISION_ID → Entry appears in log

```
| D-0001 | 2025-01-20 | BOARDROOM | Operational | Establish banking relationship with Wells Fargo | 2025-02-28 | Finance Lead | Pending | 2025-02-28 | Gate approval required before routing |
```

### 2. Status Progression (Operational Teams)

Log entry status changes as decision flows through execution:

```
Pending (Founder logged it)
  ↓
Notified (Domain received it)
  ↓
In-Progress (Domain is executing)
  ↓
Completed (Domain finished)
  ↓
Closed (Founder archived it)
```

### 3. Tracking & Accountability

Every domain can look at DECISION_LOG and see:
- What decisions apply to them
- When they're due
- What status they're at
- Any escalations needed

### 4. Audit Trail

Years later, attorney or auditor pulls DECISION_LOG and sees:
- What Founder decided and when
- Who owned execution
- Whether it was completed
- Any blockers or revisions

**This is legal evidence that the city actually ran.**

## STATUS TRANSITIONS & ESCALATION

### Normal Flow

```
Pending → Notified → In-Progress → Completed → Closed
```

### Blocked Flow

```
In-Progress → Blocked (escalation triggered)
  ↓
Escalated to Founder
  ↓
Founder decision:
  ├→ Revised (new decision issued, old closed)
  ├→ Deferred (placed on hold with new deadline)
  └→ Abandoned (closed without completion)
```

### Deadline Missed

```
If status not updated by DEADLINE:
  → ESCALATION REQUIRED
  → Escalate to Founder
  → Founder updates status or revises decision
```
