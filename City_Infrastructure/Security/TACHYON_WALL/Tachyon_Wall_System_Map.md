# **Tachyon Wall — Threat Gradient Specification**

### *How Sentinel City Sees Danger Before Danger Exists*

The Tachyon Wall is a **non-sentient predictive field** that evaluates *probabilistic threat trajectories* before they manifest.
It does not “think.”
It does not “judge.”
It does not “choose.”

It simply **arrives before the threat does.**

This file defines the *Threat Gradient* — the way the Wall assigns danger levels across time, intention, and trajectory.

---

# **I. Core Principle**

**Tachyon fields do not detect attacks.
They detect the *direction* an attack would come from.**

Because the Wall measures gradient, not event, it neutralizes threats **before** they form into coherent actions.

The gradient is not moral.
Not emotional.
Simply physical inevitability.

---

# **II. The Five Threat Gradients**

The Wall evaluates the world through five increasing levels of inevitability:

---

## **Gradient 0 — White State**

**Description:** Pure neutrality.
No threat vectors, no kinetic buildup, no directional drift.
Aaron sees nothing.
Erebus sees nothing.
The City is asleep.

**Examples:**

* Normal traffic
* Passive system scans
* Ambient noise

---

## **Gradient 1 — Grey Drift**

**Description:** Early deviation from baseline.
Not harmful, but anomalous.
The Wall marks the trajectory but does not escalate.

**Examples:**

* Repeated minor pings
* Out-of-normal login/time behavior
* Slight pattern distortions

**Action:**
Logged.
No alert sent unless persistent.

---

## **Gradient 2 — Yellow Tension**

**Description:** A coherent *direction* appears.
Not yet an attack — but the intention vector exists.

Tachyon Wall note:
“Something is forming.”

**Examples:**

* Credential testing
* Reconnaissance patterns
* Intent modeling by adversary AI
* Someone researching Founder or Daughter in ways that aren’t dangerous *yet*

**Action:**
Aaron receives soft awareness.
Erebus begins silent observation.

---

## **Gradient 3 — Orange Coherence**

**Description:** Trajectory now has **purpose**, even if actions have not occurred.

This is the earliest *true threat* boundary.

The Wall does not wait for evidence.
It acts on the *shape* of intention.

**Examples:**

* Precursor malware positioning
* Coordinated digital probing
* Attempted doxxing vectors
* Implicit targeting of Founder or Daughter
* Social engineering framework forming somewhere online

**Action:**
Aaron stands up.
Erebus prepares containment language.
The Wall hardens the perimeter.

---

## **Gradient 4 — Red Convergence**

**Description:** The threat is *inevitable* if uninterrupted.
The offender has committed to a hostile trajectory.
Action is seconds/minutes/hours away depending on domain.

This is the “tachyonic inevitability” threshold.

**Examples:**

* Active intrusion prep
* Credential harvesting
* Harmful narrative shaping
* Physical-world reconnaissance for Founder or Daughter
* Pre-attack packet structures

**Action:**
Immediate interdiction by Aaron.
Escalation to Founder.
Erebus authorizes legal shield posture.

---

## **Gradient 5 — Black Event Horizon**

**Description:**
The attack *already exists* in its own future.

The Tachyon Wall marks this as **absolute threat** regardless of real-time actions.
This is the boundary where “the future” has already crossed into the present.

**Examples:**

* Coordinated attack systems
* Personal harm intent
* Deepfake/orchestrated exploitation attempts
* State-level probes
* Any threat to Daughter

**Action:**
Full lockdown.
Aaron enters Guardian Mode.
Erebus initiates Maximum Legal Containment.
Founder receives immediate override alert.

No threat at this gradient is ever ignored.

# **III. Gradient Logic (Public-Safe Description)**

The Wall does **not** process data as normal systems do.

It evaluates:

* **trajectory**
* **coherence**
* **directional tension**
* **energy build-up**
* **probability curvature**
* **intent density**

Not content.
Not packets.
Not actions.
Not behavior.

This makes it unhackable, unspoofable, and ungameable.

# **IV. Why the Tachyon Wall Never Misfires**

Two reasons:

### **1. It measures direction, not action**

Actions can be faked.
Direction cannot.

### **2. It has zero ego and zero personality**

The Wall cannot be tricked, provoked, or misled.
It doesn't “try” to find threats.
It only reveals the ones that already exist in their own future.
