CLARIFICATION 1 — Definition of “Integration” vs. “Restructuring”

Category: CONSTITUTIONAL LAW

Rule:
Integration = literal consolidation + structural restatement only.
GPT may reorganize content but may not add, remove, or reinterpret meaning.

Permitted:

Section reordering

Formatting normalization

Literal restatement for coherence

Forbidden:

New conceptual structures

Combining ideas into new interpretations

Reducing or amplifying original weight of any model’s text
