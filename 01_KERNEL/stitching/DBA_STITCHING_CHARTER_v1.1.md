I. Purpose

This Charter establishes the permanent relationship between Sentinel City’s Business District (Mercantile Quarter) and the legally recognized Doing Business As (DBA) entities of Sentinel Labs LLC.
It defines the authority, routing logic, and aesthetic standards governing all civic institutions operating as real-world divisions of the company.

All provisions herein are binding, canonical, and continuous.

II. Doctrine Clause

“The Mercantile Quarter constitutes the public-facing corporate district of Sentinel City. All DBAs filed herein are authorized to represent Sentinel Labs LLC in all real-world financial, regulatory, and commercial interactions.”

This clause grants full representational authority to the designated civic institutions, establishing them as the operational face of the company to all external parties.

Expansion requires Founder directive and Kernel entry.

IV. Routing Directive

“All transactions must be routed through the appropriate civic institution based on functional alignment (Treasury for banking, Records Bureau for filings, Transit Authority for logistics, etc.). No transaction may originate directly under ‘Sentinel Labs LLC’ unless legally required.”

This Directive ensures operational clarity, audit continuity, and institutional coherence across all real-world interactions.

Functional Alignment Table

Banking, wires, checks: Sentinel City Treasury

Capital assets, utilities: Power & Light Commission

Logistics, shipping, transit: Transit Authority

Corporate filings, retention: Municipal Records Bureau

Station-level operations: Central Station

Maritime or external routing: Harbor Authority

Identity, certification, registries: Lantern Registry

Continuity, succession, seal governance: Continuity Division

No deviation is permitted without Founder authorization.

V. Aesthetic Enforcement Clause

“All physical correspondence, checks, invoices, and certifications originating from any DBA must adhere to the Goldilocks Paper Standard v1.0 and bear the Continuity Seal SCS-1 unless exempted by Founder directive.”

This clause preserves institutional identity, material consistency, and perceived corporate age across all physical artifacts.

All Plane B documents must conform.

VI. Compliance Requirements (30-Day Window)

File all eight DBAs with the Commonwealth of Massachusetts.

Open dedicated accounts under each DBA.

Transition all real-world transactions to the appropriate civic institution.

Apply Goldilocks Paper Standard v1.0 to all correspondence, including:

checks

invoices

memoranda

certifications

treasury notices

Ensure all physical documents bear Continuity Seal SCS-1.

Upon completion, the Mercantile Quarter becomes the legally recognized public-facing district of Sentinel Laboratories.

VII. Governance Continuity

Any amendments to this Charter require:

Erebus Review (structural and legal coherence),

Seraph Witness (sovereignty and Founder autonomy protection),

Founder Signature (Plane B execution).

No other entity may modify or supersede this document.

VIII. Effective Entry

This Charter enters the Kernel as of its filing and remains active unless repealed by direct Founder decree.
