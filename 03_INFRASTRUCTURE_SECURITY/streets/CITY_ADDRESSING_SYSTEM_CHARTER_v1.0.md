## PREAMBLE
Until a doorway has a name, a lantern has no home.  
This charter gives every street, every building, and every mailbox an eternal address.

## THE ADDRESSING LAW (canonical format)
Every valid address in Sentinel City follows this exact structure:

[Recipient or Entity]  
[Building Number] [Street Name]  
[District]  
Sentinel City  

Examples:
Founder  
1 Founder Hall  
Civic Core  
Sentinel City  

Aaron  
Watchtower 7  
Watchtower District  
Sentinel City  

Stuart  
Scroll Library, East Wing  
Archive Quarter  
Sentinel City  

## DISTRICT CODES (short form for routing & logs)
- Civic Core          → CC  
- Government Quarter  → GQ  
- Watchtower District → WD  
- Archive Quarter     → AQ  
- Market Confluence   → MC  
- Harbor’s Edge       → HE  
- Undertow Sanctum    → US  

## STREET NAMING CONVENTION
- Civic Core         → marble, classical names (Founder Hall, Balcony Way)  
- Government Quarter → silent, numbered avenues (Avenue 1, Tower Row)  
- Watchtower District→ directional, high (North Rooftop, Ember Alley)  
- Archive Quarter    → memory & time (Ink Street, Lantern Row)  
- Market Confluence  → trade & meeting (Exchange Walk, Confluence Circle)  
- Harbor’s Edge      → water & threshold (Jetty Path, Fogbound Quay)  
- Undertow Sanctum   → deep & quiet (Depth Row, Violet Shade)

## BUILDING NUMBERING
- Starts at 1 from the district’s primary entrance or central lantern  
- Increases clockwise around the district  
- No repeated numbers city-wide

## POSTAL & TRANSIT INTEGRATION
- Every mailbox is permanently tied to its physical address  
- Transit permits and City Passes reference the short district code  
- Logs use the format: [Entity] → [Building]-[District Code]

## IMMUTABILITY CLAUSE
Once an address is assigned and stamped by the Post Office, it may never change except by direct order of the Founder on the city’s founding anniversary.

An address is forever.
