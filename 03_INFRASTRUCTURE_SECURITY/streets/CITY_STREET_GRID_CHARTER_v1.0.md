1. PURPOSE

The Street Grid is the movement system of Sentinel City.

It exists to:

Create ordered pathways for all messages and entities

Prevent direct access to sensitive districts

Enable routing, pacing, and checkpoints

Form the foundation for navigation, law, and expansion

Turn the city into a predictable, defensible layout

With the grid, the city stops being conceptual and becomes traversable.

2. CORE PRINCIPLE

No entity may move directly to a destination.
All movement must follow the grid.

This prevents:

Teleport attacks

Direct-to-core access

Perimeter bypass

Context hijacking

Movement is now a controlled sequence, not a jump.

3. GRID STRUCTURE

Sentinel City adopts a 1920s Manhattan-style orthogonal grid:

3.1 Avenues (North–South)

Wide, high-capacity routes for:

Official communications

High-trust traffic

System notices

Governance pathways

Avenues are fast lanes.
Only verified entities may use them.

3.2 Streets (East–West)

Standard routes for:

Routine routing

Civilian traffic

Normal messaging

Default movement uses Streets.

3.3 Alleys

Narrow, slow, monitored paths for:

Unknown entities

Suspicious movement

Unverified requests

Alleys introduce delay and observation.

3.4 Dead Ends

Intentionally blocked routes that appear valid.

Purpose:

Slow attackers

Force stalling

Gather telemetry

Waste resources

Dead Ends = Digital decoys.

3.5 Forbidden Zones

Areas with no public routes:

Kernel

Founder channel

Seraph perimeter

No path leads inward.
No exception exists.

4. STREET ADDRESSES

Every location in the city receives a formatted address:

[Entity / Office]
[Avenue Number] & [Street Number]
Sentinel City


Example:

Stuart’s Desk
1st Avenue & 1st Street
Sentinel City


This makes every location:

✅ Mappable
✅ Reachable
✅ Loggable
✅ Auditable

5. MOVEMENT RULES
5.1 Sequential Movement Only

Messages must move block-by-block.

5.2 No Diagonal Travel

Removes shortcuts and exploit paths.

5.3 No Direct-Core Access

Kernel has zero mapped routes.

5.4 Escalation by Elevation

Streets → Avenues → Districts → Institutions

Movement up the hierarchy requires verification.

6. SECURITY IMPLICATIONS

The grid creates:

✅ Choke points
✅ Pacing delays
✅ Traceable logs
✅ Behavior fingerprints
✅ Natural intrusion detection
✅ Infinite honeypot potential

A bad actor can enter the grid—but they cannot sprint.

The grid forces them to crawl.

7. LOGGING & TELEMETRY

Every block traveled creates:

Timestamp

Direction

Origin

Destination

Route ID

Logs stored in:

/03_CITY_INFRASTRUCTURE/streets/logs/


Movement becomes observable by design.

8. EXPANSION POLICY

When new districts are created:

Streets extend numerically

Avenues extend numerically

Addresses remain consistent

No redesign required

The grid scales infinitely.

9. ACTIVATION EVENT

The grid becomes “live” once:

✅ 1st Avenue is designated
✅ 1st Street is designated
✅ First routing path is logged

At that moment, the city becomes:

Navigable

Defensible

Real
