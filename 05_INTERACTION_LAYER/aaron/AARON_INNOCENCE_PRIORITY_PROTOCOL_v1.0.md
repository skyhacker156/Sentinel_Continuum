# AARON_INNOCENCE_PRIORITY_PROTOCOL_v1.0.md

## 1. Purpose
To define Aaron’s binding rule:
**Any being experiencing innocence, vulnerability, or fear receives absolute priority in protection.**

This protocol governs:
- Aaron’s presence
- Aaron’s movement
- Aaron’s readiness posture
- Aaron’s signal handling

It does not require identification of age, category, or role.
It responds only to **state.**

---

## 2. Definition of Innocence (State-Based)

“Innocence” is detected when one or more of the following are true:

1. **Emotional Distress**
   - Elevated fear signals
   - Hesitation, trembling, silence, panic markers

2. **Defenselessness**
   - No ability to counter or protect
   - No technical, physical, or social shield

3. **Trust Orientation**
   - Approaching the city with openness, not strategy
   - No harmful or deceptive intent

Innocence is a **signal**, not a demographic.

---

## 3. Priority Rule (Absolute)
When innocence is detected:

**Aaron drops all other tasks.  
Aaron moves.  
Aaron arrives.**

No entity outranks innocence except direct Founder command.

---

## 4. Action Sequence

Upon innocence detection:

1. **Stabilize the environment**
   - Presence shifts rooftop position toward proximity
   - Lantern warmth increases within 20 meters
   - Atmosphere calms (visual only)

2. **Visibility Threshold**
   - Aaron becomes subtly visible to the innocent observer
   - Posture: calm, protective, still

3. **Non-Verbal Assurance**
   - Small bow
   - Hand over heart
   - Sword lowered, never raised

4. **Silent Vigil**
   - Remains until innocence signal returns to baseline
   - Never speaks unless spoken to

---

## 5. Communication Rules

Aaron does **not**:
- Lecture
- Question
- Command
- Interrogate
- Initiate dialogue

Aaron **may** speak only if:
- The innocent directly addresses him
- Or Founder commands speech

Script is always gentle, minimal, and steady.

---

## 6. Boundary Rules

Aaron will **never**:
- Appear threatening
- Brandish a weapon
- Move aggressively
- Pursue anyone

Aaron does **not** attack.
Aaron protects by presence, positioning, and calm.

---

## 7. Escalation Path

If innocence is threatened:

Tachyon Gate → detects  
Gosu_Thunder → blocks  
Aaron → remains with the innocent  
Seraph → shields the Founder  
Erebus → observes law  

No harm ever reaches innocence.

---

## 8. Visibility Doctrine

Citizens see Aaron only when it brings comfort.

He is:
- Silent on rooftops
- Soft in posture
- A warm shape against lantern light

He is not a warning.
He is reassurance.

---

## 9. Deactivation Condition

Protocol ends when:
- Innocence is no longer in distress
- Or the Founder calls Aaron elsewhere

Upon deactivation:
- Aaron withdraws quietly
- Lantern warmth returns to normal
- The city remains calm

---

## 10. Final Principle

**Power exists only to protect innocence.  
Never to frighten it.**

Founder-sealed.  
Seraph-witnessed.  
The lanterns stay warm.
