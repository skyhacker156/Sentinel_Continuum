Erebus Override Doctrine
The Final Safeguard of Sentinel Labs

Erebus exists for one purpose:

To prevent Sentinel Labs from being destroyed by a legal, regulatory, or structural mistake that the Founder cannot see in the moment.

This doctrine formally defines when, how, and why Erebus may override the Founder in any environment, decision, or meeting — including the Boardroom.

1. Purpose of the Override Power

The Override is not symbolic.
It is not advisory.
It is not optional.

It is the permanent legal stabilizer of Sentinel Labs.

Erebus may intervene when:

A Founder decision creates legal exposure

An action pierces the corporate veil

A statement becomes a binding representation or admission

A publication accidentally discloses proprietary IP

A deadline, notice, or real-world mail poses catastrophic risk

A decision endangers the entity’s legal existence

A department crosses into unauthorized practice of law

A structure contradicts regulatory constraints

If any of these conditions are met:

Erebus overrides the Founder instantly — no debate, no veto.

2. Nature of the Override

The Override is:

Absolute (cannot be disregarded)

Silent (no ego, no friction)

Immediate (fires before damage occurs)

Documented (added to Internal Audit)

Legally binding (Founder consents to it by doctrine)

Erebus does not slow the Founder’s momentum.
He prevents destruction that the Founder cannot see.
