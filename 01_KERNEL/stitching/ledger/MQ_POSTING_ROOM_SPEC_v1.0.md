Status: Canonical · Operational · Kernel-Linked

1. Purpose

The Mercantile Quarter Posting Room (the Posting & Interchange Annex) is the designated procedural space where:

routing slips

treasury memos

vendor notices

continuity confirmations

carbon copies

stamped drafts

inter-office communications

are prepared before posting on the Mercantile Daily Board.

This Annex establishes the institutional rhythm, friction, and chain-of-custody necessary to produce authoritative Goldilocks Documents in Plane B.

4. Posting Clerk Role (Procedural, Not Personified)

The Posting Clerk is a function, not a character.

Duties:

retrieve finalized notices

verify Annex stamp

verify routing tag

verify carbon copy presence

pin notice to the Daily Board

log posting date in the Continuity Ledger

There is no personality, identity, or autonomy — only a role.

5. Document Workflow
Step 1 — Drafting

Document produced by institution (Treasury, Records, etc.).

Step 2 — Annex Intake

Received at the Annex via in-box trays or routing orders.

Stamped:

MERCANTILE ANNEX – RECEIVED

Step 3 — Processing

Performed at the appropriate station:

typed

copied

routed

stamped

sealed (pre-seal alignment only)

Step 4 — Carbon Copy Logging

Carbon copy goes to:

/Continuity Division/Annex/Carbon Copies/

Step 5 — Posting Clerk Pickup

Document placed through the Routing Hatch.

Step 6 — Posting

Clerk pins the document to the Daily Board.

Step 7 — Founder Visibility

Documents relevant to Founder-level decisions are flagged by Seraph for review.
