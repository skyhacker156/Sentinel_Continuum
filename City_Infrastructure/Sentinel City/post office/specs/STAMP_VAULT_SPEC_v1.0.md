1. Purpose

The Stamp Vault houses all official authentication devices of Sentinel City, including:

Postmark devices

Seal dies

Date wheels

Ink pads (civic-grade)

Archival stamping tools

Retirement archive of obsolete instruments

The Vault exists to ensure:

Controlled chain of custody

Preservation of municipal standards

Prevention of unauthorized stamping

Authenticity of all stamped civic artifacts

Canonical continuity of every device used across both planes

No civic artifact may bear a mark unless the originating device is traceable to this vault.

5. Retrieval Protocol (Mandatory Sequence)

Enter vault; door closes fully behind Postmaster

Inspect humidity gauge

Unlock Stamp Storage Wall

Remove only the required drawer

Carry drawer to central table

Select device; verify stamp head integrity

Record removal in Stamp Vault Log (see Section 7)

Perform stamping duties in MQ Posting Room

Return device to exact slot

Close drawer; lock wall

Lock vault; record exit time

This ensures chain-of-custody integrity for every mark made in Sentinel City.

6. Device Calibration Rules

Every stamping device must be checked:

Weekly: Ink density

Monthly: Pressure uniformity

Quarterly: Alignment & concentricity

Annually: Brass integrity & die wear

All calibrations recorded manually in the calibration ledger.
