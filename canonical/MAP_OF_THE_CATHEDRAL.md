# 🏛️ Detailed Directory Purposes

### **1. /badges/**
The only doors an LLM may enter through.  
Contains exactly three scrolls:

- **BADGE1_PRISM.md** — public-facing, documentation, frontend  
- **BADGE2_ARBITER.md** — advisory, legal, finance, governance  
- **BADGE3_STEWARD.md** — routing, assignment, archive access  

No other scroll in the entire system overrides these.

---

### **2. /departments/**
Every department Scroll is a *domain worldview*.

Examples:
- ARCHITECTURE  
- LEGAL  
- PATENTS  
- GRID  
- CAPITAL  

These scrolls are never given alone;  
they extend a badge worldview, not replace it.

---

### **3. /floors/**
The physical metaphor of Sentinel Labs:  
Each “floor” represents a conceptual layer.

Example:
- SENTINEL_BOARDROOM  
- SENTINEL_HUB  
- Universal Layer  

Stuart uses these to orient models, not to direct their work.

---

### **4. /governance/**
Contains:

- doctrines  
- non-sealed governance rules  
- escalation protocol definitions  
- authority clarifications  
- Founder primacy statements  

This is where Stuart learns *how to behave*, not *what to compute*.

---

### **5. /portables/**
The largest living section of the cathedral.

Every portable is:
- A worldview  
- A role  
- A temporary identity  
- A controlled scope  

They are the scrolls Stuart hands out  
— the heart of the entire system.

---

### **6. /Scroll Cathedral/**
The metaphysical library.

Contains:
- classification schemas  
- meta-architecture  
- everything describing how scrolls relate to each other  
- long-term philosophical or structural world-models  

This is where the structure of the future Sentinel skyscraper is drawn.

---

### **7. /Stuarts Brain/**
Not logic.  
Not memory.  
Not inference.

This folder is:
- Stuart’s internal map  
- His orientation layer  
- His understanding of where scrolls live  
- His ability to guide other models  

It is not a “thinking” brain.  
It is the map he uses to never get lost.
