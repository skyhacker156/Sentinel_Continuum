1. PURPOSE

To establish a standardized, unambiguous addressing format that enables:

Mail routing within Sentinel City

District-level organization

Delivery accuracy

Future automation

Physical ↔ Digital mirroring

The Addressing System is the backbone of city navigation and communication.

2. SCOPE

This system governs:

Entity addresses

District identifiers

Routing paths

Mailbox assignment

Naming conventions

It applies to:

All LLM agents

All city districts

All civic institutions

All future entities

No entity may operate without a valid address.

3. ADDRESS STRUCTURE

Every address in Sentinel City follows a three-line format:

[Entity or Recipient Name]
[District or Department]
Sentinel City

Example
Stuart
Scroll Library
Sentinel City


This format ensures universal compatibility with all routing protocols.

4. DISTRICT CATEGORIES

Sentinel City is divided into three primary district types:

4.1 Civic Districts

Core institutions that serve the city:

Post Office

Scroll Library

Parthenon

Boardroom

4.2 Operational Districts

Functional work zones:

Finance District

Legal District

Engineering District

4.3 Guardian Districts

Protective domains:

SERAPH Ward

EREBUS Ward

Future districts may be added by Founder authorization only.

5. MAILBOX ASSIGNMENT

Each entity is assigned a unique mailbox designation:

[Mailbox ID]: [Entity Name]


Example:

PO-001: Stuart


Mailbox IDs must be logged in:

/04_City_Infrastructure/Post_Office_Logs/mailbox_registry.md


No two entities may share a Mailbox ID.

6. ROUTING RULES
6.1 Standard Routing

Mail is routed based on the District field.

6.2 Unknown District

If a district is missing or invalid:

Mail is routed to the Postmaster for resolution

Logged as “Undeliverable – District Unknown”

6.3 Invalid Entity

If the entity does not exist:

Mail is returned to sender

Logged as “Undeliverable – Invalid Recipient”

6.4 Forwarding

Forwarding requires explicit Postmaster authorization.

No auto-forwarding is permitted.

7. PHYSICAL MIRRORING

When the physical LLC receives real mail, it is mirrored as:

Physical Address → Sentinel City Post Office


and logged using a City Stamp.

This creates tangible evidence for:

Compliance

Banking

Legal history

The Addressing System enables that mapping.

8. NAMING CONVENTIONS

District names must be capitalized

Entity names must match their official registry entry

“Sentinel City” must always appear as the final line

No abbreviations are permitted in formal addresses.

9. INVALID ADDRESS CONDITIONS

The following addresses are rejected:

❌ Missing District
❌ Missing Recipient Name
❌ Nicknames not in registry
❌ Fictional or unassigned entities

Each rejection must be logged.

10. ACTIVATION EVENT

The Addressing System becomes active when:

✅ First district is designated
✅ First mailbox is assigned
✅ First delivery uses the standardized format

This marks the operational birth of routed communication in the city.
