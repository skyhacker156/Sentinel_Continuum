# POST OFFICE CHARTER  
### Sentinel City Edition  
**Issued by the Municipal Records & Archives Bureau**  
**Ratified: March 3, 1927**

---

## Article I — Establishment and Mandate

The Sentinel City Post Office (SCPO) is hereby affirmed as the oldest and most foundational civic institution within the municipality.  
Its enduring mandate is the orderly, secure, and timely transmission of written communications, parcels, and pneumatic dispatches for the benefit of all residents.

The SCPO shall stand as:

1. **Custodian of Public Trust**  
2. **Steward of Interdistrict Communication**  
3. **Emblem of Civic Reliability and Mechanical Excellence**

---

## Article II — Scope of Services

The SCPO is charged with maintaining and operating the following divisions:

### 1. Letter Conveyance Division  
Responsible for the daily collection, sorting, routing, and delivery of all stamped correspondence.  
Sorting procedures shall follow the Decimal Routing Index (DRI), last revised 1924.

### 2. Parcel Conveyance Division  
Handles small and medium parcels, with reinforced custody protocols and staged transfer logs maintained at each depot.

### 3. Pneumatic Dispatch Network  
Maintains the subterranean pneumatic tube system used for rapid intra-city communications.  
All junction valves must be pressure-tested during the Morning Synchronization Window (06:00–06:20).

### 4. Citizen Inquiry Counters  
Provides postage, municipal forms, public notices, and general assistance in a courteous and orderly manner.

---

## Article III — Standards of Conduct

All personnel of the SCPO shall uphold the following values:

- **Reliability:** Deliveries shall conform to posted timetables except under extraordinary civic emergency.  
- **Precision:** Routing, stamping, and logging shall be executed without haste or deviation.  
- **Courtesy:** Interactions with citizens shall remain formal, measured, and respectful.  
- **Integrity:** No correspondence shall be opened, withheld, altered, or delayed except by lawful written order from the Municipal Court.

---

## Article IV — Facilities and Civic Infrastructure

The SCPO shall maintain the following municipal facilities:

### **Central Sorting Hall**  
A marble-floored, brass-machined nexus of civic operations, illuminated by frosted globe fixtures.

### **District Letter Depots (DLDs)**  
Neighborhood distribution stations responsible for last-mile routing.

### **Pneumatic Junction Rooms**  
Restricted mechanical spaces housing pressure regulators, routing nodes, and inspection hatches.

### **Public Stamp Kiosks**  
Brass-plated kiosks dispensing official postage; serviced weekly by the Stamp Custodial Unit.

---

## Article V — Responsibilities of Citizens

Residents of Sentinel City are expected to:

1. Affix correct postage to all correspondence  
2. Include district codes and routing numbers legibly  
3. Deposit letters into designated street receptacles  
4. Conduct themselves courteously with SCPO personnel  
5. Report suspected mail tampering to the nearest depot supervisor

---

## Article VI — Historical Note

Founded during the First Expansion Era (1899), the Sentinel City Post Office has operated without interruption for more than three decades.  
Its constancy reflects the core civic belief that clear communication—honest, orderly, and dependable—is essential to a prosperous metropolis.

---

## Article VII — Amendment Procedure

This Charter may be amended by the Municipal Records & Archives Bureau following formal review and public posting, provided the institution’s principles of reliability, precision, and civic trust remain inviolate.
