Mercantile Quarter — Posting & Interchange Annex
Furniture & Fixtures Specification
Status: Canonical · Structural · Kernel-Level
Classification: Plane A Representation · Administrative Infrastructure

1. Purpose

This specification defines all furniture and fixed fixtures within the Posting & Interchange Annex.

Furniture in Sentinel City must:

reinforce municipal authority

match the architectural era (1918–1929)

support predictable workflow

enable consistent document preparation

integrate seamlessly with the Type Standard

preserve a calm, archival atmosphere

produce recognizable textures on Goldilocks Paper

The Annex is not decorative.
It is a functional administrative engine.

2. Material Standards

All furniture follows these material rules:

Primary Wood:

Quarter-sawn white oak

medium brown stain

matte finish

visible ray flecks

durable under daily administrative use

Metal Hardware:

brushed brass handles

oil-rubbed hinges

nickel foot caps where required

Fabric & Padding:

minimal

utilitarian

municipal beige

no decorative stitching

Fasteners:

Era-correct slotted screws only.
No Phillips heads.
