## **1. Purpose**

This specification establishes all stamps, seals, impressions, and pressure devices used within the Posting & Interchange Annex.

Stamps are not decorative. They are:

* routing signals
* status indicators
* timing certifiers
* archival triggers
* stitching anchors
* institutional signatures

Every stamp must reinforce Sentinel identity and conform to the **Sentinel Imprint Serif (SIS)** specification.

---

## **2. Material Standards**

### **Stamp Body**

* Vulcanized rubber (primary)
* Steel-cut brass (for long-term seals)
* Oak handles, medium-stain
* Brass ferrules attaching handle to stem

### **Ink Pad Material**

* wool-felt core
* cotton surface layer
* wooden base
* hinged lid (brass)

### **Ink Specification**

**Color:** municipal black
**Formula:**

* high carbon content
* low sheen
* fast drying
* no smearing on Goldilocks Paper
* archival-safe pigments

No blue, red, or colored inks permitted.

---

## **3. Canonical Stamp Set**

The Annex uses **nine** authorized stamps.
No others may be introduced without Founder + Seraph approval.

---

## **1. “ANNEX RECEIVED” Stamp**

### **Purpose:**

Indicates a document has officially entered the Annex workflow.

### **Format:**

```
ANNEX – RECEIVED
```

### **Typography:**

* SIS
* all caps
* 14 pt mechanical equivalent
* 3% letterspread

### **Placement:**

Top-left margin of intake slips or document’s reverse.

### **Pressure:**

Medium–light
(does not wrinkle carbon packets)

---

## **2. “POSTED – MQ BOARD” Stamp**

### **Purpose:**

Certifies a document has been posted to the Mercantile Board via routing hatch.

### **Format:**

```
POSTED – MQ BOARD
```

### **Typography:**

* SIS
* all caps
* narrow track
* heavier strike

### **Placement:**

Bottom-right of posting slip.

### **Pressure:**

Medium-heavy
(produces the signature Annex block impression)

---

## **3. Date Stamp (Mechanical Dial)**

### **Purpose:**

Applies the official Annex date.

### **Format:**

```
JAN 15 1929
```

(always uses 1920s format, even in modern day)

### **Typography:**

* SIS block
* 12 pt mechanical dial spacing

### **Placement:**

Next to Annex Received or Posting stamp.

### **Pressure:**

Light
(to avoid embossing through carbon layers)

---

## **4. “RETURN TO SENDER – ANNEX” Stamp**

### **Purpose:**

Marks documents that cannot be processed or require correction.

### **Format:**

```
RETURN TO SENDER – ANNEX
```

### **Placement:**

Centered on top margin.

### **Pressure:**

Medium

Any

---

## **4. Stamp Alignment Rules**

### **Horizontal Alignment**

± 1.5 degrees acceptable

> Anything beyond triggers a redo unless Founder override.

### **Vertical Drift**

≤ 3 mm allowed on carbon packets
≤ 2 mm on Goldilocks Paper

### **Ink Saturation**

Must be:

* even
* fully legible
* not overly wet
* not ghosted
* not double-struck

### **Overlap Rules**

No stamp may overlap:

* signatures
* SIS headers
* typewritten text
* routing grids

Overlap with handwritten notes (SLS-Fine) is acceptable.

---

## **5. Pressure & Impact Standards**

### **Light Pressure**

* date stamps
* rough drafts
* carbon assemblies

### **Medium Pressure**

* Annex Received
* Ready for Typing
* Ready for Stamping
* Copy – MQ

### **Medium-Heavy Pressure**

* Posted – MQ Board
* Archive – MQ

### **Prohibited Pressure**

Full-force stamping on Goldilocks Paper
(causes embossing ridges)

---

## **6. Stamp Placement Grid**

All documents must follow the **Annex Stamp Grid (ASG)**:

### **Primary Zones**

**Zone A (Top Left)**

* Annex Received
* Date stamp

**Zone B (Top Right)**

* Copy – MQ
* Ready for Typing

**Zone C (Bottom Right)**

* Posted – MQ Board (always here)

**Zone D (Top Center)**

* Archive – MQ

**Zone E (Left Margin)**

* Ready for Stamping

This grid ensures century-long consistency.
