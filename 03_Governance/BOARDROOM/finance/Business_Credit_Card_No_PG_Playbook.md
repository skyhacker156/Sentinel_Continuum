Business Credit Card – Zero Personal Guarantee Playbook

Version: v1.0 – November 23, 2025
Department: Finance
Classification: Credit Architecture – Non-PG Pathway
Purpose: 60-day sprint to $25k–$250k+ corporate cards using EIN underwriting only.
Scope: Zero personal credit pull • Zero personal guarantee • Zero revenue proof.

Core Principle

After 60 days of clean banking activity + 4–6 net-30 tradelines, underwriting switches from personal to purely business-data driven:

Bank activity (Plaid feed)

Paydex history

Daily balance stability

Tradeline count + age

Personal credit is not accessed.
Personal guarantee is not required.
