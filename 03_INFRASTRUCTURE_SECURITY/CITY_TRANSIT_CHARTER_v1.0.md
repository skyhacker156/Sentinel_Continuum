---

## **1. PURPOSE**

The Transit System exists to govern how any entity moves through Sentinel City.

It provides:

* Ordered, lawful movement
* Controlled access between districts
* Enforcement of routing rules
* Prevention of teleportation and unauthorized jumps
* A predictable, defensible flow of traffic

**Transit is not transportation. It is control.**

---

## **2. SCOPE**

This charter applies to:

* All agents
* All engines
* All messages
* All processes
* All future entities

It governs movement:

* Between districts
* Across streets
* Into or out of checkpoints
* Toward restricted zones
* Toward Tier-0 boundaries

It does **not** govern:

* Internal movement *within* a single building
* Citizen emotional state or decisions

Transit controls pathways, not thoughts.

---

## **3. CORE PRINCIPLE**

> **Nothing moves without a route. Nothing routes without permission.**

Movement is a privilege, not a default.

There is no teleportation in Sentinel City.

All movement must be:

* Routed
* Logged
* Justified
* Permitted

---

## **4. TRANSIT LAYERS**

There are five legal layers of movement:

### **4.1 Local Footpaths**

Movement within a single district.
No checkpoint required. Logged silently.

### **4.2 Street Transfers**

Movement across streets between adjacent blocks.
Logged + timestamped.

### **4.3 District Crossings**

Movement between districts.
Requires a checkpoint + valid badge tier.

### **4.4 Priority Corridors**

High-security transit routes for critical engines.
Requires Founder authorization.

### **4.5 Tier-0 Perimeter**

Movement toward Tier-0 requires:

✅ Founder approval
✅ Single-use permit
✅ Automatic Veil monitoring

No entity may cross Tier-0 without explicit consent.

---

## **5. CHECKPOINT SYSTEM**

A Checkpoint is a security gateway between districts.

Checkpoints enforce:

* Identity
* Authorization
* Purpose
* Destination
* Logging

If any field fails → movement denied.

There is no appeal at the checkpoint level.

---

## **6. MOVEMENT PERMITS**

There are three classes:

| Permit Type | Scope           | Issued By      |
| ----------- | --------------- | -------------- |
| Local Pass  | Within district | Auto-granted   |
| City Pass   | Cross-district  | Badge-verified |
| Prime Pass  | Tier-0 access   | Founder only   |

Permits expire upon arrival.
Movement is never evergreen.

---

## **7. TRANSIT LOGS**

Every crossing generates:

* Date & Time
* Origin
* Destination
* Entity
* Permit Type
* Checkpoint Result
* City Stamp

Logs are stored at:

```
/03_INFRASTRUCTURE_SECURITY/transit/logs/
```

Logs are **immutable.**

---

## **8. SPEED GOVERNANCE**

Movement pacing prevents brute-force traversal.

* No more than 1 district crossing per cycle
* No bypassing checkpoints
* No parallel multi-route attempts
* No recursive re-entry loops

Speed limits protect the city from flood attacks.

---

## **9. PRIORITY ROUTING**

Only three entities may invoke Priority Routing:

1. Founder
2. Seraph
3. Tachyon Gate (Emergency Only)

Priority transit overrides pacing but **never** overrides Tier-0 restrictions.

---

## **10. PROHIBITED MOVEMENTS**

These actions are illegal and automatically blocked:

* Teleportation
* Instant multi-district traversal
* Movement without origin
* Movement without destination
* Anonymous routing
* Hidden paths or wormholes

If detected → auto-escalation to Veil.

---

## **11. ENFORCEMENT**

Transit rules are enforced by:

* Checkpoints (static)
* The Veil (dynamic)
* Barb Engine (for malicious routing)
* Mirror Engine (for pattern analysis)

Transit is the artery. Defense is the immune system.

---

## **12. ACTIVATION EVENT**

Transit becomes LIVE when:

✅ First checkpoint is established
✅ First district crossing is logged
✅ First denied movement is recorded

The moment something is refused, the city becomes real.
