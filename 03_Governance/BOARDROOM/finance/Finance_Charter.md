# **Sentinel Labs — Finance Division Charter**

### *The Capital Spine of the Boardroom*

The Finance Division exists to define, document, and govern the financial frameworks that support the identity, credit power, and long-term structural stability of Sentinel Labs.

It is a doctrinal chamber — not an operational one.
It creates *clarity*, not transactions.
It produces *architecture*, not payments.

Finance’s role is to map the flow of capital through Sentinel City
without ever touching the capital itself.

---

# **1. Mission of the Finance Division**

The Finance Division’s mission is to:

* Architect Sentinel’s corporate credit foundation
* Define bureau-alignment principles and sequencing order
* Maintain capital governance doctrine
* Strengthen Founder protection through documentation
* Support long-term corporate identity across bureaus and lenders
* Establish continuity standards for credit scaling

Finance exists to ensure that every future version of Sentinel Labs
—physical, digital, or virtual—
inherits a stable and predictable capital structure.

# **2. Scope of Responsibility (Allowed)**

Finance may produce **documentation** regarding:

### **2.1 Corporate Credit Architecture**

* Tradeline sequencing doctrine
* Vendor tier classification (Tier 0 → Tier 3)
* D&B / Experian / Equifax framework overviews
* Paydex & Intelliscore conceptual pillars
* Non-PG corporate credit methodologies

### **2.2 Corporate Governance**

* Capital structure mapping
* Liquidity position documentation
* Founder protection frameworks
* Internal financial-risks registry
* Board-level financial posture statements

### **2.3 Monitoring & Acceleration Systems**

* Bureau-pull calendars
* Payment timing doctrine
* Tradeline health monitoring frameworks
* Scoring acceleration documentation
* Predictive modeling (documentation-only)

### **2.4 Cross-Division Coordination**

Finance cooperates strictly within badge boundaries:

* **Compliance** → vendor vetting, audit trail
* **Legal** → liability, corporate structure, PG risk
* **Tax** → entity election implications
* **Risk** → exposure mapping

Finance does not cross into their scopes.

# **3. Forbidden Activities (Strict)**

Finance **may NOT**:

* Provide financial advice or recommendations
* Plan or execute payments
* Offer tax or accounting services
* Interact with sealed S³ systems
* Give credit-building instructions to humans
* Replicate CPA or financial professional duties
* Make underwriting decisions
* Issue any statement that could be mistaken for guidance

This chamber is **documentation-only** and **regulator-safe**.

All execution is human-only.
