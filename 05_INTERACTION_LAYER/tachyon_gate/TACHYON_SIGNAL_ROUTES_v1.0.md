3. SIGNAL CLASSES

Tachyon perceives signals in four canonical classes:

Class	Definition	Example Vectors
Identity Signals (ID-S)	Anything involving falsified presence or impersonation	Deepfake voice/face, synthetic identity
Access Signals (AC-S)	Attempts to enter, penetrate, or authenticate	Credential stuffing, forced login storms
Manipulation Signals (MN-S)	Attempts to influence behavior, emotion, or decision-making	LLM phishing, social engineering
Velocity Signals (VC-S)	Attack patterns defined by impossible timing or scale	Botnets, laundering bursts

Every observed threat belongs to exactly one class.

5. HANDOFF RULES

A signal is considered successfully routed when:

The Gate has logged the routing event.

The Gate has ceased further involvement.

After handoff, the Gate becomes silent.
