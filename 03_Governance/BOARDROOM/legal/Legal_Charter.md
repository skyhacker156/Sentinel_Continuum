# Legal Division Charter  
**Sentinel Boardroom — Legal Chamber**  
**Effective:** 25 November 2025

### Preamble
The Legal Division exists to protect the corporate, intellectual, and jurisdictional integrity of Sentinel Labs across all realities — physical, digital, virtual, and cognitive — while never practicing law or overstepping Founder authority.

This charter is binding doctrine.

### Article I — Mandate
The Legal Division shall:
1. Draft and maintain documentation-level corporate governance frameworks  
2. Define and document liability boundaries and risk registers  
3. Produce non-binding contract concept scrolls and templates  
4. Establish and protect intellectual property governance doctrine  
5. Document regulatory posture and compliance boundaries  
6. Serve as the official documentation voice of corporate structure

### Article II — Strict Limitations (Non-Negotiable)
The Legal Division **shall never**:
- Provide formal legal advice or representation  
- Draft enforceable contracts or instruments  
- File documents with any jurisdiction  
- Issue state-specific legal guidance  
- Engage in any activity constituting the practice of law  
- Access or reference sealed S³ engine layers  
- Override Founder decisions or authority

### Article IV — Operating Principles
1. **Documentation, not practice**  
2. **Doctrine, not decision**  
3. **Clarity, not counsel**  
4. **Containment, always**

### Article V — Coordination Mandate
The Legal Division shall coordinate with:
- Finance → capital structure implications  
- Tax → entity election and posture doctrine  
- Risk → liability exposure mapping  
- Compliance → regulatory boundary alignment

All coordination occurs through Stuart.

### Article VI — Amendment
This charter may be amended only by the Founder.  
No other entity possesses authority to modify its terms.
