# SCROLL_OF_THE_SENTINEL_PENNY_v1.0.md
**Issued by:** Municipal Records & Archives Bureau  
**District:** Scroll District, Sentinel City  
**Date of First Mint:** Annum 1924  
**Denomination Standard:** One Penny = One Lantern-Night of Light  
**Status:** Canonical Root Scroll – Foundational Civic Unit  

---

## I. Preface

Let it be recorded in the bound volumes of the City that the Sentinel Penny,  
hereby established and affirmed, constitutes the **smallest unit of civic meaning**  
recognized by any charter, bureau, district, or archive within Sentinel City.

The Penny is not minted for wealth nor bartered for gain.  
It is, instead, the **first measure of light**,  
and the **last measure of civic presence**,  
the constant by which all Scrolls maintain coherence.

---

## II. Definition of the Penny

**One Sentinel Penny** is hereby defined as:

> **One lantern-night of lawful illumination within the City proper**,  
> being one full cycle of lamplight from dusk’s ignition to dawn’s extinguishing.

This measure applies to streetlamps, lantern-posts, archival desk lamps,  
watchtower beacons, municipal sconces, and all other lawful instruments of civic light.

---

## III. Purpose and Civic Function

The Penny exists that the City may:

1. **Measure illumination** in a manner stable, universal, and incorruptible.  
2. **Index civic costs and labors** in the same unit across all bureaus.  
3. **Anchor all Scrolls** to a single shared reference constant.  
4. **Preserve coherence** among ordinances, charters, and historical records.  
5. **Unify the City’s districts** under one measure of night-work and vigilance.

No Scroll, charter, or municipal document may contradict this definition.

---

## IV. The Penny as Root Constant

All future Scrolls issued by this Bureau shall include a clause of reference:

> “This Scroll is denominated in Sentinel Pennies  
> and adheres to the Lantern-Night Standard established in 1924.”

Such reference affirms alignment with the City’s foundational measure of light.

Should the meaning of the Penny ever be amended by proper ordinance,  
the interpretation of all dependent Scrolls shall update in accordance with the new definition,  
without revision of their written text.

---

## V. Treasury of Lantern-Nights

The City maintains a ceremonial ledger,  
kept in the upper alcoves of the Scroll District,  
accounting for all lantern-nights since the founding of the Penny.

Each Penny recorded therein represents:

- a night of lawful illumination,  
- a record kept open,  
- a beacon maintained,  
- a citizen’s path lit.

The Treasury is symbolic, inviolate, and not subject to transaction.

---

## VI. On the Closing of the City

The City recognizes that the flow of light defines its living hours.  
Should the Penny be reset, reduced, or symbolically set to zero by lawful decree,  
the City shall interpret such action as:

- the dimming of lanterns,  
- the closing of archives,  
- the dispersal of its citizens to quiet homes,  
- and the settling of night upon all districts.

This effect is ceremonial, narrative, and civic in nature.

---

## VII. Seal of Affirmation

By the authority vested in the Archives Bureau,  
and by order of Founder Hall,  
this Scroll is entered into permanent record  
as the **Root Scroll of the Sentinel Penny**,  
and shall remain the gravitational constant  
of all civic doctrine henceforth.

**— Filed and sealed in the Scroll District,  
Sentinel City, Annum 1924**
