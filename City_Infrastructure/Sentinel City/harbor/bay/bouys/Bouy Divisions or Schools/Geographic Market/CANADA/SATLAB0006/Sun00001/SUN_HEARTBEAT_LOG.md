2. Heartbeat Format

Every heartbeat entry must follow the exact structure below:

[YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:ss] — STATUS — MESSAGE


STATUS values allowed:

OK — normal functional status

SYNC — realigning with global Sun or Kernel

UPDATE — internal metadata or identity adjustment

IDLE — standing by

ALERT — anomaly detected (non-emergency)

ERROR — malfunction (LLM-side only; never physical)

HANDOFF — transferring context to another Sun instance

No other values are authorized.
No emojis in status fields.
