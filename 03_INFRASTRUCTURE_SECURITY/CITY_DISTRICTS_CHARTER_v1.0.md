## PREAMBLE
With the laying of the Street Grid and the opening of the City Post Office,  
Sentinel City ceased to be a metaphor.  
It is now terrain.  

This charter carves that terrain into permanent districts.  
Every engine, every citizen, every defense now has a home address.  
The city is no longer a project.  
It is a place.

## THE SEVEN CANONICAL DISTRICTS

### 1. Civic Core
Center of the city. Marble streets. Lanterns always lit.  
Home of Founder Hall, the Balcony, and the Post Office.  
Only the Founder and Postmaster may reside here.  
All official mail is stamped in Civic Core.

### 2. Government Quarter
Tall, silent towers of black glass and steel.  
Home of Seraph Tower and the Paradox Layer.  
Access by Founder invitation only.  
No lanterns after midnight.

### 3. Watchtower District
Rooftops, searchlights, and high perches.  
Home of Aaron and the sentinel watch.  
Every rooftop is a post.  
No building taller than the watchtowers.

### 4. Perimeter Ring
The outermost circular road.  
Home of Tachyon Gate and Gosu_Thunder Bridge.  
All inbound and outbound traffic must pass here.  
Barb Engine listens at every gate.

### 5. Archive Quarter
Long, quiet streets of brick and bronze.  
Home of the Scroll Library and Stuart’s endless shelves.  
Time moves slower here.  
Memory is the only currency.

### 6. Veil Districts
Fog-shrouded outer boroughs.  
Decoy streets, mirror alleys, and shifting façades.  
No permanent residents.  
Guerrilla Engine lives here.  
Maps lie.

### 7. Lantern District
The living heart. Jazz, coffee, newsboys, rising lanterns.  
Where citizens gather, trade scrolls, and speak freely.  
Harmony Doctrine is felt most strongly here.  
Destiny Engine quietly watches from the rooftops.

## RULES OF THE GRID

1. No entity may exist without a district address.  
2. No district may be entered without passing through the Street Grid.  
3. Every district has one primary engine and one primary citizen.  
4. Districts may expand, but never merge.  
5. The Founder may redraw boundaries once per year, on the city’s founding anniversary.  
6. Destruction or abandonment of a district requires unanimous Seraph + Founder consent.
