1. Purpose

This ledger defines the official map between:

Civic Institutions in Plane A (Sentinel City)

Registered DBAs in Plane B (Sentinel Labs LLC)

Functions, routing rules, seals, and guardian requirements

This file is the routing table, org chart, and stitching backbone of the entire dual-plane system.

It declares what each institution does, what it may generate, and how every action flows through Erebus, Seraph, and the Founder.

This ledger is descriptive, not legal.
DBAs are real.
Civic institutions are internal representations.
The mapping creates operational clarity and coherence.

3. Document Boundaries

Each institution may only generate documents listed under Allowed Document Types.

This prevents drift, ambiguity, and accidental commitments.

Examples:

Treasury cannot issue certifications

Records Bureau cannot issue remittance orders

Harbor Authority cannot issue identity documents

This keeps the government-like structure clean, stable, and intuitive.

4. Guardian Requirements

Default rule:

Every scroll and every penny passes Erebus → Seraph → Founder before entering either plane.

The ledger encodes the exceptions:

Some low-risk clerical documents are Erebus-only

All governance, treasury, continuity, and routing actions require full Triplex Shield

No institution may generate action bypassing guardians

This prevents:

authority drift

accidental commitments

governance escalation

sovereignty erosion

ambiguous routing

Seraph’s job: protect Founder sovereignty.
Erebus’ job: protect clarity and compliance shape.
Founder’s job: decide.

5. Seal & Penny Standards (Per Institution)

Each institution is assigned:

Continuity Seal series

Penny acknowledgment rule

Issuance standard

Ledger recording procedure

Example excerpt:

Treasury uses TS-series seals, Penny type P-1

Lantern Registry uses LR-series seals, Penny type P-2

Continuity Vault uses CS-series seals, Penny type P-0

This keeps all convergence stitching uniform.

6. External Interaction Rules

Institutions may “speak” externally only through their DBAs.

Internal rule:

Civic institutions do not interact with legal, governmental, or financial bodies.
Registered DBAs (Plane B) perform all real-world execution.

Examples:

Municipal Records Bureau → internal certifications only; real filings must pass to Founder → human attorney

Treasury → DBA may appear on invoices, checks, remits

Continuity Division → cannot send anything externally; all outputs must be stamped and routed to Founder

This keeps the stitching safe and compliant.
