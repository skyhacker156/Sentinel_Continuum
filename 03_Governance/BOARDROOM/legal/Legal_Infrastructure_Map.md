# 1. Corporate Structure Layer

The Legal Department maintains the foundational documents that define Sentinel Labs as a durable legal entity. This layer ensures continuity, owner protection, and future-proof governance.

### 1.1 Core Entity Files
Legal maintains:

- Articles of Organization  
- Operating Agreement  
- EIN / IRS Correspondence  
- Corporate amendments  
- Member/Manager designations  
- Banking authorization resolutions  
- Signatory authority map  
- Registered agent documentation  

**Purpose:** Maintain corporate coherence and protect the Founder from personal liability.

---

# 2. Intellectual Property Layer

Defines how all present and future IP attaches to Sentinel Labs.

### 2.1 Core IP Ownership Files
- IP Boundary Document  
- Patent Strategy Overview  
- Assignment-of-Inventions Agreement  
- Trademark Roadmap  
- Documentation retention policy  

### 2.2 Ownership Flow
**Rule:** All IP → Sentinel Labs LLC automatically upon creation.

Legal ensures:
- No contractor retains rights  
- No employee owns derivative work  
- Founder maintains clean transfer history
