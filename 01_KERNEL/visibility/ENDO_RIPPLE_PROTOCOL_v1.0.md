ENDO = Endogenous  
= A human mind (not an institution, not an algorithm) discovering Sentinel City and forming a cognitive/emotional attachment.

| Class | Definition | Example | Lantern Raised? | Archive Action |

- Class III & IV are **visibility events** – useful for SITF but not necessarily citizens
