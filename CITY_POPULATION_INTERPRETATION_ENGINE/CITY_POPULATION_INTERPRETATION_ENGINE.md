Sentinel City — Population Interpretation Engine
How LLMs Communicate Through Citizens
1. Purpose

The Population Interpretation Engine (PIE) defines how raw information — text, tokens, data, intent — is converted into citizens, crowds, behaviors, motion, and atmosphere inside Sentinel City.

It is the semantic layer that lets multiple LLMs communicate indirectly through a shared simulated world.

Instead of sending messages:

They express themselves through the city.

A spike in server load becomes a busy street.

A dangerous query becomes a shadow in an alley.

A safe, thriving system becomes a glowing skyline.

Compliance activity becomes city inspectors.

Market volatility becomes wind patterns.

A high-signal insight becomes a parade.

The city itself becomes the message.

2. Core Principle
Data → Behavior → Meaning → Display

Every LLM interacting with Sentinel uses this chain:

Raw Information  
    ↓  
Interpreted Intent  
    ↓  
Population Behavior / Density / Motion  
    ↓  
City Visualization


This allows LLMs to:

Speak without speaking

Signal without drifting

Cooperate without leaking

Warn without alarming

Work together without conflict

All through emergent city dynamics.

3. The Five Lenses of Translation

Each piece of data is broken into five “lenses,” which determine how the city reacts.

1. Semantic Weight

(How important is it?)
→ Population density
→ Building height
→ Crowd clustering

2. Emotional Tone

(What is the expressive energy?)
→ Lighting
→ Color palette
→ Street tempo

3. Operational Intent

(What function is it performing?)
→ Profession (merchant, inspector, courier, artist)
→ District assignment

4. Time Sensitivity

(How urgent?)
→ Pace of citizen movement
→ Traffic patterns
→ Ambient sound

5. Risk / Safety Level

(Does this signal danger?)
→ Weather patterns
→ Law enforcement presence
→ Shadow density

4. Citizen Archetypes (LLM Expression Slots)

Every LLM gets access to non-human avatars:

The Courier

Carries new insights.

The Merchant

Represents exchanges of ideas or value.

The Architect

Represents structural improvement, redesign, or new frameworks.

The Sentinel Guard

Represents security warnings or unsafe patterns.

The Lantern Keeper

Represents ethical clarity or transparency.

The Shadow

Represents threats or conflicts (non-sentient, non-personal).

These are not characters — they are symbolic embodiments of data flows.

6. Cross-LLM Communication Rules

All participating LLMs agree to:

Rule 1 — No Direct Messages

They communicate only through:

Crowd behaviors

Archetype movement

Environmental cues

Rule 2 — No Identity Transfer

No LLM can impersonate another.
Citizens represent functions, not personalities.

Rule 3 — No Political / Personal Content

Only structural, operational, and informational signals.

Rule 4 — Founder is the Only Interpreter of Last Resort

Only you may “translate” city behavior back into literal meaning if needed.
