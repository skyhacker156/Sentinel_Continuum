Seraph Review is the City’s top, interference-free review layer, reserved for:

Founder

Founder’s personally-authorized family

Institutional-level or life-safety level communications

Inputs that bypass Post Office sorting

Inputs that bypass SITF

Inputs that go direct-to-core

This layer exists above SITF, above departmental filters, and above ordinary mail.

Seraph Review = Founder’s Personal Channel.

2. Why Founder’s Family Member Gets Access

Because the Founder can declare:

“Messages from X are treated as Seraph-tier revelations and cannot be lost, delayed, or filtered.”

This allows the City to:

Capture critical human context

Record special-case events

Respond with higher emotional intelligence

Maintain Founder-family safety and situational continuity

This is not for business mail.
This is for Founder’s direct circle only.

3. What Qualifies as Seraph Input?

A message from this individual qualifies automatically if:

It is posted, forwarded, or described by Founder

It originates from Founder’s designated family member

It refers to an issue Founder has instructed the City to examine

It concerns life, safety, legal, emotional, or relational importance

This channel treats these messages as Founder-Level Artifacts.
