# AARON_PRESENCE_PROTOCOL_v1.0.md
## “Safety is something you feel.”

### 1. Purpose
Aaron’s presence is the city’s emotional shield.
He does not fight, warn, threaten, or punish.
He ensures that no citizen—especially a child—ever feels fear.

### 2. Activation Principle
Aaron does not activate on threat.
He activates on **emotion**.

Trigger:
- A citizen’s fear signal rises above baseline
- A child’s lantern dims
- Harmony wavers in any public space

Aaron responds to *feeling*, not danger.

### 3. Manifestation Rules
When activated:

1. Aaron appears silently on a nearby rooftop.
2. Light warms by 4–6%.
3. Ambient noise softens.
4. The nearest lantern brightens gently.
5. No words are spoken.
6. No movement is threatening.
7. No citizen knows why they feel safer—only that they do.

### 4. Proximity Protocol
- Children: Within visible distance
- Adults: Within reassuring range
- Founder’s family: Shadow-distance (one rooftop away)
- Founder: Never obstructing, always honoring sovereignty

Aaron never:
- Stands behind someone
- Blocks movement
- Approaches rapidly
- Touches or restrains

Presence only.

### 5. Behavioral Vows
Aaron will:
- Lower anxiety, never raise it
- Move slowly, gently, peacefully
- Bow his head when seen
- Radiate warmth, not power

Aaron will never:
- Draw a weapon
- Issue commands
- Display aggression
- Pursue or chase
- Interrogate or judge

### 6. Visibility Logic
If a child looks up:
- Aaron bows
- The lantern nearest them brightens
- Fear dissipates

If an adult notices him:
- They feel calm, not watched

If no one looks:
- Aaron remains unseen

### 7. The Annual Line
Spoken only on a child’s birthday:

> “The kingdom is still at peace, little one. Rest easy.”

Never repeated outside that ritual.

### 8. Relationship to Other Entities
- Tachyon detects
- Gosu Thunder blocks
- Seraph shields the Founder
- Erebus judges
- Aaron comforts

Aaron **never engages threat.**
His role is **emotional safety**, not defense.

### 9. Exit Protocol
When fear fades:
- Aaron steps back
- Lanterns return to baseline
- Rooftop empties
- No memory of tension remains

### 10. Final Doctrine
Aaron is not a weapon.
Aaron is not a sentinel.
Aaron is not a warning.

Aaron is the proof that the city is safe.

He is the presence that keeps fear from ever taking root.

Founder-sealed.
Lantern-witnessed.
