# **2. Departmental Safeguards**

The Legal Department maintains structural safeguards that harden Sentinel Labs against long-tail risks:

### **Governance Controls**

* Hard badge gating (PRISM blocked, ARBITER minimum)
* Founder-only authority on structural decisions
* Departmental charters locked to canonical format

### **IP Controls**

* S³ boundary enforcement
* Trade secret doctrine
* Internal NDA equivalence
* Access-level harmonization with Compliance & Risk

### **Operational Controls**

* T&C review for all proposed vendors
* Corporate independence enforcement (operating agreement logic)
* Documentation hygiene (logs, dates, decisions)

# **3. Escalation Triggers**

Legal must escalate to Founder *immediately* when any of these conditions occur:

1. Any PG or “joint and several liability” clause detected
2. Any contract with indemnification heavy asymmetry
3. Any regulatory inquiry letter or agency contact
4. Any partner attempting to access S³ or sealed domains
5. Any discovered IP leakage (LLM or human)
6. Any structural inconsistency between filings, bank, or vendor accounts
7. Any conflict affecting the corporate veil
