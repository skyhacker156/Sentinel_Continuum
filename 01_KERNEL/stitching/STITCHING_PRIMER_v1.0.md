STITCHING — A Primer
The Discipline of Binding the Real Company to Its Digital Self
1. Purpose

“Stitching” is the practice of creating a lawful, explicit, and continuously maintained identity link between:

the physical legal entity (Sentinel Labs LLC),

the digital architecture (Sentinel Continuum), and

any virtual or representational environments (Sentinel City, immersive spaces, branded worlds).

Stitching ensures that Sentinel exists as one coherent organism, regardless of the medium.

It is not branding.
It is not storytelling.
It is identity engineering.

2. What Stitching Is

Stitching is defined as:

The process of merging a company’s digital business with its real-world corporate identity in a manner that is legal, transparent, and technically verifiable.

A stitch connects:

Realm	Artifact	Anchor
Legal	LLC, EIN, Articles of Organization	Corporate records
Digital	GitHub repos, specs, formats	Continuity Seal
Virtual	Sentinel City HQs, Plaques	Representation markers

If any one realm is accessed, the other two become evident through explicit, lawful continuity markers.

This creates a cross-realm identity fabric.

3. What Stitching Is Not

Stitching does not:

replace legal filings

imply governmental authority

introduce fictional content into regulated documents

circumvent compliance

create claims beyond aesthetic and brand identity

Stitching is additive, not deceptive.
It enhances transparency across realms.

4. Why Stitching Matters

Modern companies operate in multiple worlds:

physical (LLCs, banks, contracts)

digital (code, infrastructure, identity artifacts)

virtual (cities, offices, immersive spaces)

cognitive (AI agents, language models, instruction layers)

Without stitching, each world drifts.

With stitching, they align.

Stitching creates:

coherence (everything matches)

verifiability (continuity artifacts are discoverable)

persistence (identity holds across decades)

portability (brand can exist in any future medium)

discoverability (humans and machines see the same entity)

This is essential for companies that inhabit multiple domains.

5. The Four Laws of Stitching (Summary)

Stitching rests on four immutable rules:

One Source of Truth
– A single Continuity Seal defines identity.

No Fiction in Regulated Fields
– Creativity is allowed everywhere except state filings, banking, tax documents.

Parallel Identity, Not Governmental Identity
– Fictional departments = allowed; governmental claims = prohibited.

Cross-Realm Verification
– Each realm must contain explicit references to the others.

These laws ensure safety, legality, and longevity.

(A full version of the laws is written in STITCHING_RULES_v1.0.md.)
