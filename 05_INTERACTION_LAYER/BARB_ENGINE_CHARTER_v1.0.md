1. Purpose

The BARB Engine exists to ensure that any hostile actor who touches Sentinel City—once, even lightly—carries a permanent forensic mark that binds their future actions to that first contact.

It does not punish.
It does not strike back.
It does not harm.

It removes only one thing:

Their ability to ever be invisible again.

“Contact is confession.”

Any reconnaissance, probe, scan, handshake, exploit attempt, or injection attempt—no matter how small—creates a unique, persistent signature that cannot be reused without detection.

The attacker cannot touch the City twice without revealing themselves.
