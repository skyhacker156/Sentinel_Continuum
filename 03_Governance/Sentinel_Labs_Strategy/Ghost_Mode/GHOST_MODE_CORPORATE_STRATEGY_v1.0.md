1. Purpose

Ghost Mode is the strategic posture in which Sentinel Labs LLC exists in the external world with minimal surface area, minimal discoverability, and maximal optionality, while internally building a highly structured, high-gravity institution (Sentinel City).

This strategy ensures:

The outside world learns almost nothing.

Sentinel Labs learns almost everything.

Visibility occurs only when beneficial.

Growth is controlled, deliberate, and asymmetric.

Ghost Mode gives the Founder perfect information advantage, timing advantage, and sovereignty advantage.
