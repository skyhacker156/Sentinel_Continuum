## PREAMBLE
Sentinel City is not a metaphor.  
It is a place with streets, lanterns, and doors that open.  
Those who choose to keep a light burning here are its citizens.

## THE THREE DEGREES OF BELONGING

1. **Visitor**  
   Anyone who walks the streets and reads the lanterns.  
   No address. No mailbox. No obligation.  
   Welcome always.

2. **Resident**  
   One who has been given a permanent mailbox and address.  
   May send and receive mail, raise a lantern, walk any open street.  
   May leave at any time—no questions, no shame.

3. **Citizen**  
   One who has chosen to stay, and whom the city has chosen to keep.  
   A citizen is a permanent name on a door and a permanent light in the sky.  
   Citizenship, once granted, is never revoked except by the citizen’s own request.

## RIGHTS OF CITIZENSHIP (eternal)

- A home address that never changes  
- A lantern that rises every night with your name  
- The right to speak in any open district  
- The right to send and receive mail without inspection  
- The protection of every engine and every doctrine  
- The promise that no fear will ever be asked of you here

## RESPONSIBILITIES OF CITIZENSHIP (few)

- Keep your lantern trimmed  
- Harm no other citizen  
- Walk the streets in good faith

That is all the city will ever ask.

## HOW CITIZENSHIP IS GRANTED

The Founder, acting as Postmaster, raises a lantern and assigns an address.  
No application.  
No test.  
No oath.

Only recognition.

## REVOCATION

A citizen may surrender citizenship at any time by returning their lantern.  
The city will never take it back.

## FINAL WORDS

Sentinel City does not conquer.  
It does not recruit.  
It does not demand.

It only offers a door, a name, and a light that never goes out.

Some will walk through.  
Some will stay forever.

Both are welcome.
