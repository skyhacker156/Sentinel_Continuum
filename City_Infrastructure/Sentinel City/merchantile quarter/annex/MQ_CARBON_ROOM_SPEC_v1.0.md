

# **MQ_CARBON_ROOM_SPEC_v1.0.md**

**Mercantile Quarter Annex – Carbon Room Specification**
**Status:** Canonical • Active • Plane A Institutional Architecture
**Placement:** MQ Annex (adjacent to Posting Room)

---

## **1. PURPOSE**

The Carbon Room exists to create, register, and preserve all duplicate copies of documents produced in the MQ Annex.

It is the **memory organ** of the Mercantile Quarter.

All carbon copies created here serve three functions:

1. **Institutional Continuity** — duplicate records ensure no posting or notice is ever lost.
2. **Auditability** — carbon packets form the daily MQ Annex log.
3. **Inter-Agency Dispatch** — copies are forwarded to the Municipal Records Bureau, Treasury, and relevant institutions as required.

No notice may be considered complete until its carbon copy is produced and logged here.

---

## **2. ROOM ARCHITECTURE**

The Carbon Room is directly adjacent to the Posting Room on its **west wall**, accessible via a 32″ oak-framed archway.

**Dimensions:**

* 12 ft × 14 ft
* 10 ft ceiling height
* Same stone tile flooring as Posting Room for continuity
* Walls: milk-paint cream with horizontal beadboard wainscoting (36″ height)

**Lighting:**

* Two overhead frosted glass fixtures, warm incandescent
* A task lamp fixed at each carbon press station

**Noise Profile:**

* Dampened and quiet; only the faint scratch of stylus and soft clack of ledger boards

---

## **3. FURNITURE & FIXTURES**

### **Carbon Press Tables (x2)**

* Oak, 42″ wide
* Felt underlay to prevent bleed-through
* Fixed iron alignment rails for exact sheet positioning

### **Carbon Paper Drawers**

Located on the north wall:

* Drawer A: Black carbon–ledger grade
* Drawer B: Blue carbon–interoffice grade
* Drawer C: Red carbon–Treasury priority
* Drawer D: Fresh stock (sealed, monthly rotation)

### **Binder Shelf**

South wall, three rows:

* Row 1: Daily Carbon Binders
* Row 2: Weekly Consolidation Binders
* Row 3: Monthly Archive Boxes (clothbound, stamped MQ)

### **Tool Rail**

Mounted above Press Tables:

* Alignment stylus
* Carbon spatula
* Ribbon knife
* Corner square
* Ledger hole-punch (2-hole municipal standard)

### **Routing Hatch (Carbon Output)**

Small brass-framed hatch leading into Posting Room’s filing alcove.

Used to hand off completed carbon packets.

---

## **4. PAPER & MATERIALS**

### **Primary Stock**

* Goldilocks Paper duplicates (second sheet)
* SIS carbon sheets (municipal grade)

### **Carbon Combination Protocol**

Standard carbon packets are arranged:

1. **Top:** Typed original (from Posting Room)
2. **Middle:** SIS carbon sheet (color depends on destination)
3. **Bottom:** Goldilocks duplicate sheet

Carbon color coding:

* Black → MQ Internal Copy
* Blue → Municipal Records Bureau
* Red → Treasury / Financial institutions

---

## **5. STAMPS USED IN THIS ROOM**

Stamps applied **only** to carbon copies:

* **COPY – MQ** (Zone E) — required for all MQ-retained duplicates
* **ROUTING – M.R.B.** — blue carbon copies only
* **ROUTING – TREASURY** — red carbon copies only
* **CARBON COMPLETE** (small SIS rectangular) — top-right corner of the packet

No posting stamps, no date stamps, no board stamps apply here.

---

## **6. WORKFLOW (CLOCKWISE)**

The Carbon Room workflow mirrors the same clockwise principle used throughout MQ Annex operations.

### **Step-by-step sequence:**

1. **Intake from Posting Room**
   Notice arrives via handoff from Posting Room after typing but before stamping.

2. **Carbon Packet Assembly**
   Clerk selects carbon sheet based on required routing.

3. **Alignment**
   All sheets placed under iron alignment rails.

4. **Carbon Impression**
   Clerk uses stylus to trace key fields (title, date, amount fields).

5. **Verification**
   Duplicate lifted, checked for clarity at task lamp.

6. **Stamping**

   * COPY – MQ (Zone E)
   * Routing stamp (if applicable)
   * CARBON COMPLETE (top-right)

7. **Binding**
   Entered into Daily Carbon Binder (chronological order).

8. **Routing**
   Blue/red copies slid through Routing Hatch for inter-agency transfer.

9. **Archive Placement**
   MQ copy remains on Row 1 (day-of), later consolidated weekly.

---

## **7. DAILY RHYTHM**

Carbon work follows these fixed windows:

* **09:00–10:00** — Morning carbonization of early notices
* **13:00–14:00** — Midday duplication batch (most Posting Room outputs)
* **16:00–16:30** — Final daily reconciliation (binders locked and shelved)

All clerks operate quietly; accuracy is prioritized over speed.

---

## **8. CANONICAL RULES**

1. No notice is valid until the carbon copy is created.
2. No carbon copy is valid until stamped COPY – MQ.
3. Blue/red copies must pass through the Routing Hatch the same day.
4. MQ copies must be bound by day’s end.
5. No new tools or stamps may be introduced without Founder directive.
6. Type Standard SMS-1923 governs all handwritten annotations (minimal).
7. Carbon Room and Posting Room must remain architecturally consistent.

---

## **9. RELATION TO OTHER ROOMS**

* Receives originals from **Posting Room**
* Sends duplicates to **Ledger Room** (once built)
* Sends inter-agency copies via Routing Hatch
* Provides daily packets for **Municipal Records Bureau**

The Carbon Room is the **memory spine** of the MQ Annex.
