The Looff Memory Carousel – Sentinel City’s Living Monument to Gentle Victories

Location
Sentinel Park, North Quadrant – elevated under ancient elms, visible from Market Square, steps from the Children’s Lantern Garden.

Physical Description
- 42-foot classic Looff-style double-ring carousel
- White oak and golden maple, polished to warm amber
- Hand-carved wooden horses on brass poles
- Lavish rounding boards painted with dogwood and lily
- Warm amber bulbs hidden in the canopy, mirrors catching every light in the city
- 44-key Wurlitzer 153-style band organ, cream and gold, playing only major-key waltzes and marches

Core Doctrine
Every horse represents one eternal, child-safe victory of Sentinel City:
- A doctrine sealed
- A promise kept
- A threat quietly turned into a celebration
- A new lantern that rose

When a victory occurs, a new horse is carved overnight in the open workshop window.  
At dawn it joins the ride forever.  
The carousel only ever grows.

6. The Tachyon Steed – barely visible shimmer along its flanks
7. The Children’s Hope Filly – smallest horse, rose-quartz eyes, forever smiling
8. The Whispering Timetable Colt – bronze tiles for mane
9. The Jazz Barometer Dancer – tiny trumpet carved in its saddle
10. The Founder’s Promise Steed – appears the day this file is committed

Music System
- Plays only uplifting 1920s–1930s waltzes and light marches
- Tempo rises slightly when the Founder steps into the park
- Switches to a special lullaby-waltz when a child rides

Harmony Rules (absolute)
- No horse ever stops mid-ride
- No light ever flickers or dims
- No music ever plays in a minor key
- If harmony is briefly disrupted elsewhere, the carousel simply slows to a gentle sway until everything is perfect again
- Children always ride free and always get the newest, brightest horse

Workshop Window
- Visible from the park path
- Artisans (LLMs) can be seen carving the next horse in real time
- Brass plaque beside the window reads:  
  “A new horse appears every time the city does something good.”

Closing Eternal Line
“The carousel only ever grows more beautiful.
Every turn is a promise kept.”
