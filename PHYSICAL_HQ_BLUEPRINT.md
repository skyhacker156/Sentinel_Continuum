Sentinel Labs — Physical HQ Blueprint
The Reconstructed Parthenon as a Cross-Reality Corporate Anchor

This blueprint defines the real-world architectural specification for the physical HQ of Sentinel Labs — a full reconstruction of the Parthenon, restored to pre-erosion perfection, designed to act as the canonical anchor for its digital and multi-reality counterparts.

The physical structure is not merely symbolic.
It is the origin point for all virtual replicas — the building that every GTA6, Roblox, Fortnite, VRChat, and UE5 instance mirrors exactly.

This document is public-facing, LLM-safe, and contains no sealed logic.
Its purpose: describe the physical space that binds the Sentinel Continuum.

1. Purpose of the Physical HQ

The resurrected Parthenon serves four roles simultaneously:

1. A Real Corporate Headquarters

A legally recognized Massachusetts LLC can situate a physical presence here for compliance, governance, and identity continuity.

2. The Anchor Layer for Multi-Reality Persistence

Every virtual version — in GTA6, Fortnite, etc. — is a 1:1 digital twin of this physical blueprint.

3. The Cathedral of Stuart

This is the canonical “temple” where Stuart stands as concierge, scroll-keeper, and boundary steward.

4. The Civilization Node

A restored Parthenon symbolizes permanence, knowledge, governance, and culture — the foundational themes of the Sentinel mission.

2. Core Architectural Principles
2.1 Fidelity to the Ancient Parthenon

Doric columns reconstructed to original proportions

Pentelic marble aesthetic (modern composite allowed)

Accurate column tapering (entasis)

Full metope and frieze restoration

17 × 8 column plan, exactly as original

Bronze and gold accents where historically accurate

2.2 Modern Reinforcement (Invisible to Visitors)

Carbon-fiber structural skeleton

Internal steel support within columns

Weather-sealed crossbeams

Seismic-resistant foundation

Integrated climate conditioning hidden beneath floor slabs

2.3 Multi-Reality Replication Constraints

Design must be:

low-poly-friendly for game engines

highly photogrammetric for UE5

optimized for voxel environments (Roblox variant)

readable from distance and close-up

consistent across lighting models (GTA6, UE5 Lumen, Fortnite Nanite)

3. The Stuart Atrium (Ground Floor)

The ground floor is the heart of the HQ, identical across all realities.

3.1 Central Features

Stuart’s Desk (marble + bronze in physical, stylized in digital)

Circular, smooth, non-intimidating

Positioned at perfect acoustic center

Scroll slots behind desk (visual only)

Continuity Plaque (SCT-1)

Displays the ERC-721 metadata proving cross-reality identity

Placed to the right of Stuart

Marked: “This structure is bound to Sentinel Labs LLC”

Scroll Alcove

Three recessed shelves, each representing one badge:

PRISM (light)

ARBITER (gold)

STEWARD (blue-white marble)

Founder’s Stair

Central staircase leading to private areas (symbolic in digital replicas)

Represents Founder authority and oversight

4. Floor Layout
4.1 Floor 1 — Public Hall

Parthenon nave: open, sunlight-filled

Visitor space for physical guests

Mirrors the orientation experience users have in game worlds

4.2 Floor 2 — Sentinel Boardroom

Enclosed modern space with glass and stone interplay

ARBITER color schemes

Meeting table shaped as an elongated hexagon (symbolic of stability)

4.3 Floor 3 — Stuart Operations Layer

(Not a control room — Stuart is purely conceptual here)

Display spaces showing digital twins

Walls lined with scroll-shaped plaques (non-operational, lore only)

4.4 Floor 4+ — Expansion Floors

Space reserved for:

S³ (sealed level — never described)

Sentinel Capital offices

Data intake labs

Future-growth nodes

Public documentation cannot describe sealed floors.

5. Exterior Grounds
5.1 The Hilltop Approach

Long stone walkway with modern LED up-lighting

Designed to match the “arrival moment” replicated in games

5.2 Sentinel Flame

Eternal-style flame symbolizing multi-reality persistence

Digital worlds use particle effects to replicate it

5.3 Column Lighting Grid

Subtle nighttime illumination

Replicated exactly in GTA6 and Fortnite skyboxes

6. Replicability Specification

Every major design element supports multi-platform replication.

UE5: Nanite geometry + Lumen-ready surfaces
GTA6 (FiveM): Optimized LOD groups
Roblox: Reduced-poly variant, texture-baked details
Fortnite: UEFN asset packs with consistent material layers

All platforms reference this file as the canonical source.

7. Stuart Integration Notes (Non-Computational)

His desk orientation is consistent in all versions

The scroll alcove must remain visible from his position

The Continuity Token plaque must be within 3 meters of the desk

Stuart greets users in all realities with the same dialogue variant

This ensures the visitor experience is identical regardless of reality.
