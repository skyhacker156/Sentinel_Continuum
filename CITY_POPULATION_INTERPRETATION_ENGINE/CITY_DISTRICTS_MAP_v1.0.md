Sentinel City – Official Districts Map (26 November 2025)

The city is laid out as a perfect 1920s–1930s metropolis radiating from the Parthenon Summit.

1. Parthenon Summit (North Hill)  
   The marble balcony, Stuart’s eternal post, the Grand Elevator shaft.  
   No streets, no fog, no noise. Only wind and coffee.

2. Sentinel Park (North Quadrant)  
   Ancient elms, Children’s Lantern Garden, Looff Memory Carousel, soft grass paths.  
   The city’s gentle heart.

3. Market Square (Central Plaza)  
   Jazz Barometer rooftop, newsboys, crowds, lanterns, heartbeat of the city.

4. Finance District (Broad Street / Wall Street)  
   Finance Tower, ticker-tape parades, brass-band celebrations when capital flows.

5. Post Office Square (5th & Main)  
   The great art-deco Post Office, pneumatic tubes, eternal memory vaults.

6. Harbor District (Eastern Waterfront)  
   Docks, gentle silver mist, dockworkers who pause when new lanterns rise.

7. Newspoint Alley (connects Finance to Market Square)  
   Flying headlines, shouting newsboys, the city’s artery of stories.

8. Tachyon Wall Outpost (Western perimeter – invisible to visitors)  
   Soft shimmer only badge-holders can see.

9. Children’s Lantern Garden (adjacent to Parthenon path)  
   Floating lanterns, Aaron’s quiet watch, perfect safety zone.

10. Rooftop Patrol Paths (continuous across downtown)  
    Aaron’s silent silver walkway, visible only at dusk.

Layout Notes
- All streets are lit by warm gas-style lamps that never flicker.
- Every district is within a 5-minute walk of Market Square.
- No dead ends, no shadows, no locked gates for citizens with badges.
- The city grows outward in perfect concentric rings as new districts are born.

Closing Constant

Every light is a promise kept.”

Visitors can stand on it and always find their way home.
