## **1. Purpose**

This document defines the **daily rhythm** of the Posting & Interchange Annex:
the exact sequence, timing, cadence, and conditions under which administrative actions occur.

A predictable rhythm is essential for:

* stitching consistency
* document lineage
* stamp timing
* routing reliability
* archival discipline
* guardian awareness
* Goldilocks fidelity
* institutional quiet authority

Time is one of the Annex’s tools.

---

## **2. Operating Hours**

### **Opening:** 8:00 AM

### **Closing:** 4:00 PM

No night operations.
No extended hours.

This fixed schedule reinforces municipal discipline and eliminates ambiguity in routing timestamps.

---

## **3. Daily Timeline (High-Level)**

1. **8:00–8:20**
   Opening procedures

2. **8:20–9:00**
   Intake processing

3. **9:00–11:30**
   Sorting, typing, carbon assembly

4. **11:30–12:00**
   First Posting Cycle

5. **12:00–1:00**
   Midday quiet period (lights dimmed)

6. **1:00–3:00**
   Second work cycle (typing, stamping, routing)

7. **3:00–3:30**
   Final Posting Cycle

8. **3:30–4:00**
   Filing, archival prep, room reset

This rhythm does not drift.

---

## **4. Opening Procedures (8:00–8:20)**

Performed in strict order:

### **8:00**

* Lamps turned on (pull-chain sequence: left → center → right)
* Pendant lights switched on
* Windows cracked open one inch
* Stamp Press Table brushed clean

### **8:05**

* Ink pads checked
* Date stamp advanced
* Typewriters tested (one-line strike)

### **8:10**

* Intake Tower emptied
* Internal Annex Envelope tray checked
* Carbon sheets replenished

### **8:15**

* Cubbies reset
* Routing slips restocked
* Alignment Cards stacked in Stamp Corner

### **8:20**

Annex is operational.

---

## **5. Intake Processing (8:20–9:00)**

All incoming documents from prior evening or morning delivery enter the workflow:

### **Step 1: Date Stamp**

Applied lightly in Zone A.

### **Step 2: Annex Received Stamp**

Medium pressure, top-left.

### **Step 3: Intake Slip Attached**

Clerk initials in SLS-Fine.

### **Step 4: Initial Sorting**

Placed into one of three cubbies:

* AWAITING TYPING
* AWAITING STAMPING
* RETURN TO SENDER (if incomplete)

### **Step 5: Routing Slip Assignment**

If document is actionable.

All intake must be completed by 9:00 AM.

---

## **6. Morning Work Cycle (9:00–11:30)**

This is the Annex’s highest-output period.

### **Typewriter Work (9:00–10:30)**

* All Type A and Type B forms typed
* Carbon packets assembled
* Headers typed in SMS-1923
* Postings prepared for the first cycle

### **Stamping Work (10:30–11:30)**

* Ready for Stamping
* Ready for Typing
* alignment checks
* carbon packet sealing
* Posting Slip attachment

Documents ready by 11:30 proceed to First Posting Cycle.

---

## **7. First Posting Cycle (11:30–12:00)**

### **11:30 exactly**

Posting Wagon loaded.

### **11:35–11:50**

Documents verified:

* Posting Slip attached
* Stamp alignment checks
* Routing Slip complete

### **11:50–12:00**

Documents passed through Routing Hatch to the Mercantile Posting Board.

Time window is fixed.
Anything not ready waits for 3:00 cycle.

---

## **8. Midday Quiet Period (12:00–1:00)**

* Pendant lights dimmed
* Typewriters idle
* Stamp Press unused
* Windows closed
* Minimal foot traffic
* No posting or stamping allowed

Annex quiet periods protect:

* guardian clarity
* institutional tone
* error reduction
* stamp pad longevity
* paper handling stability

This hour is sacred.

---

## **9. Afternoon Work Cycle (1:00–3:00)**

### **1:00–2:00**

Typing, stamping, routing continues.

### **2:00–2:30**

Carbon copy assembly for second batch.

### **2:30–3:00**

Final alignment and verification.

Documents intended for the 3:00 cycle must be Present & Complete by 2:55.

---

## **10. Final Posting Cycle (3:00–3:30)**

### **3:00**

Annex clock chimes softly (mechanical).

### **3:02**

Wagon loaded.

### **3:10–3:25**

Posting verification.

### **3:25–3:30**

Last transfer to Posting Board via Routing Hatch.

This cycle closes the day.
No postings occur after 3:30.

---

## **11. Closing Procedures (3:30–4:00)**

### **3:30–3:40**

* All trays cleared
* All cubbies reviewed
* Return to Sender items sorted

### **3:40–3:50**

* Typewriters covered
* Stamp pads closed
* Ink cleaned from stamp bodies
* Lights dimmed to half-level
* Windows latched

### **3:50–4:00**

* Daily Archive Box sealed
* Label applied (SLS-Fine)
* Placed in Filing Cabinet Drawer 1 (ARCHIVE – CURRENT MONTH)

At 4:00 PM precisely, Annex becomes inactive.

---

## **12. Weekly Rhythm (High-Level)**

### **Monday**

* Heaviest intake
* New carbon sheets stocked
* Stamp pad replacement checks

### **Wednesday**

* Routine calibration of:

  * typewriters
  * stamp pressure
  * alignment rails

### **Friday**

* Archive transfer preparation
* Vault envelope checks
* End-of-week Posting Board review

---

## **13. Monthly Rhythm**

### **Last business day of month**

* Monthly Archive Box sealed
* Transferred to Continuity Division
* Unused forms destroyed
* New Type A/B stock rotated in

Time-based discipline is an anchor of institutional continuity.
