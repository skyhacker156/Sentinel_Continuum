6. SECURITY LAYERING

The floorplan itself enforces security:

A. Line of Sight Protection

From the doorway, no sensitive board is directly visible.
The desk bank shields the Cipher Table.

B. No Window Rule

Prevents external observation.
Matches 1920s intelligence rooms.

C. Dual-Key Door

Key 1: Founder

Key 2: SITF (stored in vault)

Both required.

D. Equipment Wall (East)

All devices are to the right upon entering —
mirroring FBI and MI5 office layouts to standardize physical workflow.
