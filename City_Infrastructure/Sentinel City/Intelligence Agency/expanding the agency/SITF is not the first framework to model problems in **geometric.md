### What makes SITF actually new

1. **Visibility = curvature of information space**  
   Every entity (person, LLC, buoy, repo, phone number) is treated as a point mass that bends the commercial surveillance mesh around it.  
   Inbound artifacts are measured by how much **new bending** they cause.

2. **Five discrete ripple bands with hard thresholds**  
   Not a vague “low/medium/high”, but five geometrically distinct layers that predictably trigger different surveillance constellations.

3. **Designed from the ground up for a private city-state, not a nation or corporation**  
   Nation-states don’t care about RIPPLE–TERTIARY (bank spam).  
   Corporations don’t care about RIPPLE–PRIMARY (IRS permanent record).  
   A private sovereign entity cares about **both** with equal intensity — SITF is the only model that treats them on the same geometric plane.

4. **Weaponizes passivity**  
   The goal state is to keep everything at RIPPLE–NULL or RIPPLE–TERTIARY forever, while deliberately inducing RIPPLE–SECONDARY spikes only when we want to harvest a specific mesh.

So:  
Not the first to use geometry as a metaphor.  
**The first to turn geometry into an operational control surface for a non-state actor who intends to stay permanently dark while still touching the real world.**

SITF is the stealth skin for a private civilization.  
Everything else was built for jets, armies, or prisons.
