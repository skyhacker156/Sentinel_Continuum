# MUNICIPAL_STREETLAMP_ORDINANCE_1924  
**Enacted by Resolution of the Common Council**  
**Filed with the Municipal Records & Archives Bureau**  
**Sentinel City, March 4th, 1924**

---

## **SECTION I — PURPOSE AND SCOPE**

Be it ordained that this Ordinance is hereby established for the regulation, installation, operation, and upkeep of municipal streetlamps within the incorporated boundaries of Sentinel City.  
It is the intent of the Council that every public thoroughfare be illuminated in a manner consistent with the City’s standards of safety, civility, and mechanical dignity.

This Ordinance supersedes all prior streetlamp provisions, directives, and informal practices.

---

## **SECTION II — THE FROSTED GLOBE STANDARD (FGS–1924)**

Beginning July 1st, 1924, all municipal streetlamps installed, refurbished, or otherwise brought into active service shall conform to the *Frosted Globe Standard (FGS–1924)*, defined as follows:

1. **Globe Composition:**  
   Each lamp shall employ a spherical enclosure of tempered glass, uniformly frosted to a minimum opacity of *Grade 3*, as recognized by the Power & Light Commission.

2. **Diameter:**  
   The globe shall measure *14 inches* in diameter, with a permissible variance of no more than *½ inch*.

3. **Light Diffusion:**  
   Frosting shall disperse illumination evenly within a radius of *45 feet* from the lamp’s base, with no harsh glare detectable beyond *30 feet*.

4. **Mounting Collar:**  
   The globe shall attach via the approved brass mounting collar, FGS–1924 Type B.

No lantern, globe, or lighting assembly failing to meet these definitions shall be permitted for street-facing use after the compliance date.

---

## **SECTION III — HEIGHT AND FIXTURE REQUIREMENTS**

To maintain uniform illumination and uphold the civic aesthetic of Sentinel City:

1. **Standard Height:**  
   Lamps shall stand *14 feet, 6 inches* from ground level to the apex of the globe.

2. **Corner Exceptions:**  
   Major intersections may employ lamps of *16 feet*, provided all fixtures at that intersection share the same height.

3. **Foundation Footing:**  
   Each lamp shall be secured by a *cast-iron base* anchored at least *24 inches* below grade.

4. **Armature Details:**  
   Support posts shall be riveted steel with a brushed finish, free from decorative filigree unless approved by the Art & Beautification Board.

---

## **SECTION IV — ILLUMINATION WINDOW**

1. **Daily Activation:**  
   Lamps shall illuminate at **one-half hour past sunset**, as determined by the Observatory at Founder Square.

2. **Nightly Deactivation:**  
   Lamps shall extinguish **one-half hour before sunrise**, except during inclement weather or emergencies as directed by the Commissioner of Public Safety.

3. **Seasonal Adjustments:**  
   The Power & Light Commission may implement timing adjustments not exceeding *five minutes per week*.

---

## **SECTION V — MAINTENANCE AND INSPECTION**

1. **Quarterly Inspection:**  
   All lamps shall be inspected no fewer than *four times annually* by certified Commission personnel.

2. **Globe Replacement:**  
   Cracked, darkened, or irregularly frosted globes must be replaced within *72 hours*.

3. **Electrical Element Check:**  
   Elements shall be inspected for wear and luminosity compliance with FGS–1924 requirements.

4. **Base Cleaning:**  
   Lamp bases shall remain free of rust, grime, or unauthorized postings. Such postings must be removed within *24 hours*.

5. **Repair Response Time:**  
   Non-functioning or significantly dimmed lamps must be restored to full function within *48 hours* of report.

---

## **SECTION VI — CITIZEN RESPONSIBILITIES**

Residents and visitors share in maintaining the dignity of municipal lighting:

1. Report extinguished, damaged, or flickering lamps to the City Hotline.  
2. Refrain from attaching posters, placards, or notices to lamp posts.  
3. Maintain clear passage around lamp bases.  
4. Follow directions issued by maintenance crews.

---

## **SECTION VII — PROHIBITED ACTS & PENALTIES**

### **Prohibited Acts**
1. **Tampering:** Loosening bolts, removing globes, or impairing electrical components.  
2. **Defacement:** Painting, etching, or discoloring any part of a lamp.  
3. **Unauthorized Modification:** Installing non-standard bulbs or replacing frosted globes with clear alternatives.

### **Penalties**
- **First Offense:** Fine up to *$25*.  
- **Second Offense:** Fine up to *$75* and referral to the City Magistrate.  
- **Third Offense / Malicious Damage:** Fine up to *$150* and potential civil liability for repair costs.

---

## **SECTION VIII — EFFECTIVE DATE**

This Ordinance shall take effect **July 1st, 1924**, and shall remain in force until amended by resolution of the Common Council.
