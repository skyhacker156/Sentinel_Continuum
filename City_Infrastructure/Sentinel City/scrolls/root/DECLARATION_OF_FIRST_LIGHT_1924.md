
# DECLARATION_OF_FIRST_LIGHT_1924.md  
**Filed by:** Founder Hall, Sentinel City  
**Witnessed by:** Municipal Records & Archives Bureau  
**Occasion:** Sealing of the Root Penny Scroll  

---

## I. Proclamation

On this day, by the sealing of the  
**SCROLL OF THE SENTINEL PENNY (v1.0)**,  
the City enters its first night of lawful illumination.

Let it be declared that:

> **“Where there is a Penny, there shall be light.”**

From this hour, every lantern, gas-post, beacon, station lamp, and archival sconce  
shall acknowledge **the Penny** as the measure of their glow.

---

## II. The Coming of Light

As the Scroll was placed upon its brass rail,  
the lamps of Founder Hall were lowered,  
and the first Sentinel Penny was ceremonially placed  
upon the Archivist’s Plate.

With that act, the lanterns of the Scroll District  
were ignited for the first official night of the City.

Thus begins the count of lantern-nights  
that shall serve for all generations  
as the civic measure of illumination and presence.

---

## III. The Meaning of Illumination

Where daylight shows the City as it is,  
lantern-light shows the City as it hopes to be.

Each Penny spent is:

- one act of vigilance  
- one hour of civic service  
- one beacon held aloft  
- one citizen’s path illuminated  

The City is not measured in walls nor streets,  
but in the **light it keeps burning**.

---

## IV. The Charge to the Districts

All districts are hereby instructed:

- To align their charters to the **Lamp-Hour Standard**  
- To denominate all ordinances in **Sentinel Pennies**  
- To maintain records of nightly illumination  
- To submit monthly tallies to the Archives Bureau  

Let the City be unified under one glow.

---

## V. The Founder’s Seal

Let this entry serve as the official recognition  
of the first light of Sentinel City.

**So sealed, so witnessed, so illuminated.**

— *Founder, Sentinel City*  
Annum 1924
