---

## 1. Purpose

A **Convergence Stitch** establishes a single canonical artifact whose identity is simultaneously valid in:

- **Plane A** (Sentinel City’s civic framework), and  
- **Plane B** (Sentinel Labs LLC’s real-world operations).

A Convergence Stitch eliminates the dual-version problem.  
There is no “digital version” and “physical version.”  
There is only **one artifact** authenticated through two independent trust models.

---

## 2. Definition of a Convergence Stitch

A Convergence Stitch exists when all of the following conditions are met:

1. **Unified Identity:**  
   The artifact possesses a single authoritative identity across both planes.

2. **Bidirectional Legibility:**  
   Humans interpret the physical expression;  
   LLM systems interpret the structured digital expression;  
   Both resolve to the same meaning.

3. **Integrity Anchors:**  
   The artifact contains the required Sentinel integrity markers:  
   - Continuity Seal (CS-series)  
   - Guardian Review Metadata (Erebus + Seraph)  
   - Sentinel Penny Acknowledgement  

4. **Plane-Neutral Formatting:**  
   The document is formatted such that its digital and physical representations  
   are recognizably the same object.

5. **Routing Compliance:**  
   It has passed the full Guardian Pipeline:  
   **Erebus → Seraph → Founder Approval.**

A Convergence Stitch is not symbolic.  
It is the operational standard that ensures alignment across two domains.

---

## 3. Required Components

A valid Convergence Stitch must include:

### 3.1 Continuity Seal  
A persistent identifier binding the artifact to the Continuity Registry.

### 3.2 Guardian Metadata  
Non-executing review lines confirming:  
- Erebus: Compliance and clarity review  
- Seraph: Sovereignty and authority review  
- Founder: Final approval

### 3.3 Sentinel Penny Acknowledgement  
A ledger entry indicating issuance, registry, or validation.

### 3.4 Goldilocks Paper Standard (Physical Plane)  
For physical manifestations, the document must be printed on  
120 gsm ivory paper with wide margins and institutional formatting.

### 3.5 Canonical Markdown (Digital Plane)  
The digital version must be rendered as stable markdown with  
identical content to the physical document.

---

## 4. Governance Rule

**Rule:**  
_No artifact may enter Plane A or Plane B until it is fully converged._

The identity of the artifact must be final and immutable before:

- adoption in Sentinel City (Plane A), or  
- transmission to real-world counsel, vendors, or agencies (Plane B).

There are no exceptions.

---

## 5. Trust Model Alignment

A Convergence Stitch creates a dual-validation system:

### 5.1 Human Trust Layer (Physical)
Humans validate through:  
- tactile paper  
- embossed seal  
- ink signature  
- institutional design  
- DBA and masthead alignment

### 5.2 LLM Trust Layer (Digital)
LLMs validate through:  
- text structure  
- hash integrity  
- continuity seal ID  
- guardian metadata  
- routing line  
- penny ledger entry

Both layers converge on the same artifact  
without the need for translation or separate versions.

## 7. Enforcement

No guardian, officer, agent, or automated system may bypass this standard.  
All artifacts must demonstrate convergence before:

- being stored in the registry,  
- becoming civic law,  
- being printed on Goldilocks paper, or  
- being transmitted to any real-world party.

Only the Founder may authorize exceptions.

---

## 8. Persistency Clause

A Convergence Stitch remains canonical until explicitly revoked or superseded by a higher-order charter bearing:

- the Continuity Seal,  
- Guardian metadata, and  
- the Founder's ink signature.

Absent these three elements, all revisions are invalid.
