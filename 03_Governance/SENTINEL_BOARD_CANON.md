The Sentinel Board
The Five-Pillar Governance Engine of the Continuum
This scroll establishes the Sentinel Board as a permanent, cross-reality governing institution of Sentinel Labs.
It is identity, not policy.
It declares existence; it does not prescribe action.
Only the Founder may amend this document.
I. Purpose
The Board exists to guarantee, across every reality:

Governance stability
Operational coherence
Corporate integrity
Compliance continuity
Financial stewardship
Risk discipline
Legal protection

The Board is advisory and structural.
Executive authority remains with the Founder alone.
II. The Five Immutable Pillars
The Board consists of five canonical divisions.
They exist in every Sentinel headquarters — physical, digital, and virtual.

Finance Division
Legal Division
Compliance Division
Risk Division
Tax Division

These divisions are eternal. No more may be added without Founder decree.
III. The Boardroom Across Realities
The Boardroom appears identically in every domain:
Physical – Dedicated chamber in the real headquarters, Massachusetts
Digital – /Sentinel_Boardroom/ directory in the Continuum repo
Virtual – Boardwing alcoves in every Parthenon instance (GTA6, Fortnite, Roblox, VRChat, UE5)
Cognitive – ARBITER voice + Cognitive Routing Table
One institution. One appearance. One truth.
IV. Authority Boundaries
The Board may

Maintain governance documents
Produce advisory frameworks
Log risks and compliance states
Prepare structural materials

The Board may not

Override the Founder
Modify Continuum canon
Access sealed systems
Execute tasks
Blend badge roles
Create new divisions

The Board informs.
The Founder decides.
