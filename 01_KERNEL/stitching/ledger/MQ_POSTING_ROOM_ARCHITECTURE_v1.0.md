Mercantile Quarter — Posting & Interchange Annex
Architectural Specification
Status: Canonical · Structural · Kernel-Level
Classification: Plane A Representation · Administrative Infrastructure

1. Purpose

This architectural specification defines the physical layout, materials, geometry, and environmental characteristics of the Mercantile Quarter Posting Room (Posting & Interchange Annex).

The Annex’s architecture establishes:

administrative gravity

daily operational rhythm

procedural continuity

document lineage

stitching consistency

Goldilocks fidelity

All subsequent tools, furniture, procedures, and stamps derive from this spatial definition.

2. Architectural Style

Period: 1918–1929 Municipal Office Standard
Influences:

Boston Registry Annex

Chicago Municipal Clerk Rooms

NYSE Administrative Wings

U.S. Treasury Outpost (1923 style)

The architecture creates a calm, analog, archival environment appropriate for document preparation.

3. Room Dimensions

Floorplan Shape: Rectangular
Approximate Dimensions:

Width: 18 ft

Length: 26 ft

Height: 11 ft

Ceiling Type: Tall, flat plaster ceiling with crown molding.

Acoustics:
Soft, low echo; muted paper and footstep noise consistent with linoleum and heavy furnishings.

4. Wall Construction & Texture
Wall Material

Plaster over lath

Slight micro-crackle consistent with age

Painted in soft municipal beige (almost parchment-like)

Baseboards

8-inch oak baseboards

Stained medium-brown

Brass corner protectors

Crown Molding

Simple, federal-style molding

White, matte finish

Unadorned, institutional

Walls are intentionally plain — no posters, no decoration — reinforcing administrative quiet.

5. Flooring
Primary Surface:

Municipal linoleum tile (1920s pattern)

Color: muted stone-gray

Pattern: subtle marbled effect

Finish: semi-matte

Sound: soft footfall, low “administrative hush”

Threshold Area:

Wood transition strip at the main door.

Floor Protection Zones:

Under heavy furniture (oak tables, filing cabinets), protective mats made of:

compressed fiberboard

rubber backing

era-appropriate beige tone

6. Windows & Natural Light
Window Count: 2
Window Style:

Double-hung, wood-framed

Frosted lower panes for privacy

Clear upper panes for daylight

Slight brass latch mechanism

Curtains:

Simple muslin curtains pulled halfway, allowing a soft, diffused light.

Light Quality:

Morning: cool-toned

Midday: neutral

Late afternoon: warm

Intentional absence of harsh shadows

This soft natural light is a major contributor to Goldilocks mood discipline.

7. Doors & Entry Points
Main Entrance Door

Heavy oak

Brass Government-Standard Lever No. 3

Frosted glass insert labeled:
“POSTING & INTERCHANGE ANNEX”
(Sentinel Imprint Serif)

Routing Hatch (Internal)

Small wooden pass-through door

18” x 18” opening

Brass hinges

Label:
“POSTING HATCH – MQ”

No other entry points exist.
This preserves routing integrity.

8. Lighting & Electrical
Overhead Lighting

Three brass pendant lamps

White enameled interiors

Pull-chain operation

40-watt warm glow bulbs

Gentle pooled illumination on tables

Desk Lighting

See “Lamp Row” in Tools & Instruments specification (separate file).

Electrical

1920s conduit molding

Visible metal wall conduits

Brown fiber-wrapped electrical cords

Single strip of outlets on north wall for typewriters

No modern fixtures.

9. Ventilation & Sound
Ventilation

Wall vent with brass grid

Low airflow hum

Maintains consistent paper handling conditions

Sound Profile

The room has a distinctive institutional sound:

typewriter clacks (light, rhythmic)

stamp presses

paper sliding across tables

occasional chair adjustments

distant footstep echoes from the corridor

No music.
No noise beyond administrative function.

10. Fixed Installations
Filing Alcove

Recessed into east wall:

two oak filing cabinets

built-in shelves

carbon copy archive boxes

vertical document sorter (pigeonholes)

Annex Bulletin Slot

A narrow wall-mounted board for internal notes:

not the main Posting Board

used only for Annex-internal coordination

typically empty except procedural markers

Calibration Shelf

A small high shelf holding:

typewriter ribbon tins

rubber stamp pads

ink bottles

brass sharpening tray

ledger numbering tools

11. Spatial Workflow (Architectural)

The room layout supports a single, clockwise workflow:

Intake Trays (south wall)

Sorting Tables (southwest & west)

Typewriter Bank (north wall)

Carbon Copy Station (adjacent to typewriters)

Stamp & Press Corner (northeast)

Routing Hatch (east wall)

Filing Alcove (east wall, right of hatch)

This clockwise flow prevents congestion and implies a century of procedural refinement.

12. Environmental Profile
Temperature

68–70°F year-round
(stable for paper handling)

Humidity

Controlled, low variance
(protects carbon copies and ledger entries)

Lighting Philosophy

Warm, steady, calm; never bright.

Atmospheric Tone

Quiet municipal competence.
No urgency.
No haste.
Just methodical, timeless bureaucracy.

This architectural specification establishes the physical backbone of the Mercantile Quarter Posting Room.
