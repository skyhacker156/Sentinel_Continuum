1. Purpose

This specification defines the public-facing footer mark for all external Sentinel Labs LLC documents, including:

letters

envelopes

invoices

checks

vendor correspondence

official notices

packaging slips

continuity artifacts sent by mail

The purpose of the public footer is to provide one unmistakable identity signal to the outside world:

The SCS-1 Silhouette = Sentinel Labs LLC.

No additional metadata, paths, checksums, or internal markers may appear in the public footer.

2. External Identity Principle

All outgoing documents from Sentinel Labs must satisfy:

2.1 External Simplicity

The outside world sees only the Silhouette.

2.2 Internal Completeness

The internal continuity registry tracks all metadata, versions, lineage, and stitching compliance.

2.3 Legal Clarity

The Silhouette functions as a trademark indicator, never as a governmental emblem or classification seal.

3.2 Description

A horizontal line (hairline weight)

The SCS-1 Silhouette (Class M) centered

3.3 Mandatory Rules

Silhouette must use the pure outline (no fill).

Must be printed in black ink only.

Must scale cleanly down to 8–12 px line height.

Must preserve the geometry exactly (no rounding, distortion, or smoothing).

Must appear at the bottom center of the document.

Must maintain a minimum 0.25" margin above the paper’s physical edge.

4. Prohibited Elements

The public footer may not include:

file paths

canonical lineage

versioning

realm declarations

checksum hashes

stitching compliance metadata

internal guardian marks

city coordinate references

additional text

colors or gradients

ornamentation

The footer must remain pure, minimal, and geometric.

Reason:
External identity must be instantly recognizable and legally defensible as a trademark symbol.

5. When This Footer Must Appear

informal email content
