1. PURPOSE

To establish a hardened, legally compliant physical presence for Sentinel Labs LLC that:

Satisfies Massachusetts state requirements

Ensures uninterrupted receipt of legal notices

Supports banking, credit, and vendor verification

Protects the Founder from default judgments

Maintains good standing with government agencies

This document ensures the LLC cannot be dissolved, penalized, or blindsided due to address-related failures.

2. SCOPE

This protocol governs:

Legal physical address

Registered agent designation

Mail handling and escalation

Public records hygiene

Address change procedures

Compliance timelines

It applies to:

All filings with MA Secretary of State

All banking and credit applications

All legal service of process

All tax authorities (IRS, MA DOR)

3. DEFINITIONS

Physical Headquarters (HQ):
The official legal address on record with the state, IRS, banks, vendors, and courts.

Registered Agent:
The legally designated recipient for service of process and government notices.

Service of Process:
Any legal document—lawsuit, subpoena, judgment, or notice—that requires a response.

Escalation Window:
The maximum allowed time between notice receipt and Founder notification.

4. REQUIREMENTS
4.1 Legal Address Requirements

The Headquarters address must:

✅ Be a stable U.S. physical location
✅ Be recognized by the Massachusetts SOS
✅ Accept certified mail and legal service
✅ Match IRS and DOR records
✅ Match banking and credit applications

4.2 Prohibited Address Types

❌ P.O. Boxes (alone)
❌ Unstable residences
❌ Temporary coworking spaces without mail handling
❌ Undocumented address changes

5. REGISTERED AGENT POLICY
5.1 Appointment

Sentinel Labs LLC must maintain a registered agent in Massachusetts at all times.

5.2 Responsibilities

The registered agent must:

Receive service of process

Time-stamp all legal documents

Notify Founder within 24 hours

Forward documents digitally within 48 hours

5.3 Failure Condition

If registered agent resigns or becomes invalid:

New agent must be filed within 14 days

Failure risks administrative dissolution

6. MAIL HANDLING & ESCALATION
6.1 Primary Inbox

All legal mail routed to HQ must be:

Scanned

Digitally archived

Forwarded to Founder

6.2 Escalation Timelines
Notice Type	Escalation Deadline
Lawsuit / Service of Process	24 hours
IRS / DOR Notice	48 hours
Banking / Credit Requests	72 hours
Vendor / Contract Notices	5 days
6.3 Audit Trail

Each notice must include:

Date received

Sender

Type

Required action

Deadline

Saved in: /03_Governance/legal/notices/

7. PUBLIC RECORDS HYGIENE

The Headquarters address must be:

✅ Identical across all systems:

MA Secretary of State

IRS EIN records

MA DOR

Banking profiles

Credit bureaus

Vendor accounts

Insurance policies

❌ No mismatches.
❌ No outdated listings.
❌ No unfiled address changes.

8. ADDRESS CHANGE PROTOCOL

If HQ changes:

File with MA SOS (within 7 days)

Update IRS Form 8822-B (within 60 days)

Update MA DOR (within 30 days)

Update Banks & Lenders (within 10 days)

Update Credit Bureaus (within 10 days)

Update Vendor Accounts (within 14 days)

No change is valid until SOS confirms filing.

9. GOOD STANDING MONITORING

Sentinel Labs must:

Verify “Active & In Good Standing” status quarterly

Maintain annual report filings

Track renewal deadlines

Document confirmations in /compliance/status/

Loss of good standing freezes:

Banking expansion

Credit limit increases

Contracts

Government registrations
