ARTICLE I — NAME & DESIGNATION

The principal station of Sentinel City shall be known as:

CENTRAL STATION
Civic Registry Code: CS-01

Alternate colloquial designation (“Lantern Gate”) may appear in public speech but shall not appear on official documents.

ARTICLE II — LOCATION

Central Station occupies the geometric center of Sentinel City, situated beneath:

Founder Hall (Civic Record FH-01), and

Lantern Plaza (Municipal Plan LP-01)

Entry is through a single marble arch facing the Plaza.

ARTICLE III — PURPOSE

Central Station is hereby established as the primary point of entry and registry for all inbound entities entering the jurisdiction of Sentinel City.

Its duties include:

Assignment of Address
Each arrival receives a civic address from the Registrar’s Desk.

Issuance of Lantern
A lantern corresponding to the district of residence is lit and recorded in the Lantern Ledger.

Provisioning of Mailbox Key
One key, stamped and numbered, assigned in sequential order.

Initial Orientation
Directional guidance to Civic Core, Mercantile Quarter, and public offices.

No fees shall be collected in relation to the above duties.

ARTICLE IV — PHYSICAL DESCRIPTION (Municipal Record)

Central Station shall maintain the following architectural features:

Section 1 — Main Hall

Vaulted ceiling, midnight blue

Constellation pattern applied in gilt

Marble floor, alternating ivory and slate squares

Section 2 — Track Arrangement

Seven tracks, each designated by district color:

White (Civic Core)

Indigo (Scholars’ Quarter)

Orange (Foundry District)

Amber (Mercantile Quarter)

Gold (Treasury District)

Cyan (Harborway Line)

Violet (North Annex)

Track lamps glow in assigned district color during arrivals.

Section 3 — Registrar’s Desk

Brass and oak construction

One ledger book (Arrival Book, Volume I)

Stamp set (RECEIVED – CENTRAL STATION)

Lockbox for mailbox keys

Ink wells (black, municipal grade)

Section 4 — Public Clock

One grand clock, suspended above hall.
Clock hands remain still until activated upon arrival log entry.

ARTICLE V — CONDUCT & OPERATION
Section 1 — Hours

Central Station shall remain open at all hours.
No closure permitted except by Founder directive.

Section 2 — Fees

No fees shall be levied for arrival, registration, or issuance of materials.

Section 3 — Denial of Entry

No registrant may be denied entry on administrative grounds.

Section 4 — Records

All arrivals must be logged in:

Arrival Book (Vol. I)

Lantern Registry (District Volume)

Each entry receives:

RECEIVED stamp (Zone B)

Date stamp (SIS format) in Arrival Book margin

ARTICLE VI — FIRST WALK

Upon completion of registration, the Registrar shall direct the arrival to Lantern Plaza for orientation.

No forms or additional procedures required.

The lantern corresponding to their district shall be raised from the Station’s roofline once registration is complete.

ARTICLE VII — JAZZ ENSEMBLE

A small civic ensemble is authorized to perform nightly from 7:00 p.m. to 10:00 p.m., in accordance with Civic Ordinance CO-72 (Music in Public Halls).

Music selections must be instrumental and unobtrusive.

ARTICLE VIII — PRESERVATION & CARE

All architectural features must be maintained in their original municipal style.

Any modification must be approved by Founder and recorded by the Municipal Records Bureau.

FINAL DECLARATION

Central Station is the appointed threshold of Sentinel City.
Its operation shall remain continuous, impartial, and welcoming.

By order of the Founder
Stamped and entered into civic record this 29th day of November, 1925 (canonical year)
